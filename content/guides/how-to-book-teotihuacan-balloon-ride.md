Booking a Teotihuacán balloon ride is easy once you know what the ticket includes. The date is rarely the problem. Picking the right basket, arranging transport from Mexico City before dawn, getting a clear confirmation and knowing what happens if the weather keeps the balloons on the ground take more thought.

Tourist flights take off at sunrise in the Teotihuacán valley, northeast of Mexico City. You can book a seat in a shared balloon, a private basket for your group, or a package with transport, breakfast and time at the archaeological zone. Listings for these often use the same photos, but they are different products.

The operator policies quoted below were checked on 2 November 2026. Prices, pickup times and terms change, so rely on the written conditions of your own booking.

> **Quick answer:** Choose a shared or private flight, decide whether you need round-trip transport from Mexico City, compare the total price and the cancellation terms, then book with the operator or on a marketplace. After paying, check the confirmation for the date, passenger names, meeting point, check-in time, what is included and what happens in bad weather. A weather cancellation does not always get you a full refund: several operators offer a new date only.

## Step 2: Compare the total price, not just the headline ticket

Two tours with similar headline prices can end up far apart. One includes breakfast and insurance; another charges separately for transport, photos or a visit to the archaeological site.

Before paying, check each of these:

1. **Flight fare:** is the price per person, per basket or per group?
2. **Pickup and return transport:** included or sold separately? Is there a surcharge outside the central pickup area?
3. **Breakfast:** included, optional or not offered?
4. **Insurance:** is passenger cover included, and does it require registration?
5. **Photos or video:** included files, an optional purchase or a separate photographer?
6. **Archaeological zone:** guided or on your own? Is entry included?
7. **Taxes and payment fees:** will the price change at checkout or if you ask for an invoice?
8. **Weather cancellation:** which part of the payment comes back if the flight cannot go?

Booked direct, [WE FLY's price page](https://weflyteotihuacan.com/teotihuacan-balloon-prices/) shows shared flights from MXN 1,990 per person and lists Mexico City transport as an optional add-on, free within 4 km of the Angel of Independence. [HappyFly's 2026 page](https://happyfly.mx/en/prices/) lists a shared flight at MXN 2,100 per person with buffet breakfast. Those are the operators' own peso prices. The marketplace listings on this site are priced in US dollars and usually bundle pickup and breakfast: the {{< tour-link tour="357659P1" >}}flight with cave breakfast, pyramids and Mexico City pickup{{< /tour-link >}} costs $148 per person (4.9/5 from 8,211 reviews). Check the fare and what it covers at checkout.

### A simple way to compare packages

Write down the whole trip, not just the balloon fare:

**Total trip cost = balloon tickets + transport + meals + attraction entry + optional extras + fees.**

This matters most when you compare a cheap flight-only ticket with a dearer package that also drives you back to Mexico City after the visit.

## Step 3: Decide how you will get to the launch area

Teotihuacán balloons do not take off in Mexico City. They launch near the archaeological zone in the State of Mexico, roughly 45 to 50 km by road from the city, and operators expect you there very early.

You have three common choices.

### Book a ticket with a Mexico City transfer

For many visitors this is the simplest choice. Transfers can be shared or private. Some operators pick you up at your hotel; others ask you to meet at a central point. Never assume hotel, Airbnb or door-to-door pickup.

[Vuelo en Globo's terms](https://vueloenglobo.mx/teminos-y-condiciones) (in Spanish), for example, set the shared-transport departure point at the Ángel de la Independencia and state that the van does not collect passengers from private homes or Airbnbs. Other operators work differently: WE FLY's price page offers free hotel pickup within 4 km of the Angel.

Ask for the actual pickup location and where you will be dropped off, not just whether a listing says "Mexico City transfer". Our guide to [getting to the Teotihuacán balloon port](/guides/how-to-get-to-teotihuacan-balloon-port/) compares the routes.

### Book a flight-only ticket and arrange your own ride

This works if you have a reliable early-morning driver or are staying near the launch fields. Use the operator's own meeting point from your booking; several balloon companies fly from different sites in the area.

Don't go to the entrance of the archaeological zone unless the operator tells you to. The check-in desk, or *globopuerto*, is usually somewhere else. Our [flight-only page](/teotihuacan-balloon-flight-only/) lists tickets without transport.

### Stay near Teotihuacán the night before

A night near the launch fields takes the stress out of a very early start. It suits families, anyone who dislikes pre-dawn transfers, and travelers with a tight schedule the next day. Compare the hotel cost with the price of a return transfer; our page on [where to stay for a Teotihuacán balloon ride](/where-to-stay-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/) covers the options.

## Step 4: Book directly or use a tour marketplace

Both work, and each has its own advantages.

| Booking directly with the operator | Booking via a marketplace |
|---|---|
| You can ask detailed questions about the balloon and launch site | You can compare formats, reviews and packages in one place |
| You may be able to arrange group transport or extras | Many listings allow free cancellation up to 24 hours before the start |
| The operator handles changes on the day directly | The marketplace may be your payment and refund contact, even though the operator flies you |
| The price can be lower, but not always | The price may differ from the operator's own offer |

Neither is always better. A marketplace's free-cancellation label applies only to the listing you bought: on Viator listings marked free cancellation, you get a full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before the start, and the {{< tour-link tour="696822" >}}GetYourGuide balloon flight with optional pyramids tour{{< /tour-link >}} has the same 24-hour rule. Booked direct, operators set their own terms, typically 48 to 72 hours (see the table below). An operator's general FAQ is also not always the policy attached to every promotion.

Before paying, find out who is selling the ticket, who runs the flight and whom you contact if plans change. A marketplace ticket can involve two businesses with different responsibilities.

## Step 5: Choose your date and book far enough ahead

Balloon flights depend on the morning weather and on operator capacity. If you have several days in Mexico City, fly early in your trip, so a postponed flight still leaves you another morning.

[WE FLY's FAQ](https://wefly.com.mx/en/faq/) recommends booking at least one or two weeks ahead, especially for weekends and holidays, and at least a month ahead for long weekends and 14 February. That is one operator's advice, not a rule for the valley. Last-minute bookings can work, but they leave you less room, and some operators do not allow date changes on bookings made within 72 hours of the flight.

For a short visit, a practical sequence is:

- **Before the trip:** compare packages, restrictions and weather policies.
- **Several days or more before the flight:** reserve a date if your plans are firm.
- **The day before:** check the operator's latest pickup and check-in instructions.
- **On flight day:** arrive early with your photo ID and confirmation.

For a private basket or a celebration package, confirm the number of passengers, any extras and the exact total before paying.

## Step 6: Check your flight confirmation carefully

A successful payment does not prove every detail was recorded correctly. Save the confirmation email, the e-ticket, any WhatsApp messages and the payment receipt.

Your confirmation should answer all of the following:

- **Operator's name** and the booking reference or ticket number.
- **Flight date** and passenger count.
- **Passenger names** exactly as the operator requires.
- **Shared or private flight**, including any minimum group size.
- **Check-in location** with a map link or full address.
- **Check-in deadline** and, if relevant, **pickup time and place**.
- **What is included:** breakfast, transfer, photos, insurance, entry fees and other extras.
- **Amount paid**, any balance due and accepted payment methods.
- **Contact number** for the morning of the flight.
- **Cancellation, rescheduling and refund conditions** for this booking.

Don't forget identification. [WE FLY's check-in rules](https://wefly.com.mx/en/politicas-y-restricciones/) require a valid government-issued photo ID for every passenger, and a card-paying customer must show the card used for the purchase. Without ID you cannot board, although WE FLY lets you reschedule. Other operators have their own rules, so check yours.

### Sample message to send before booking

If the listing leaves anything unclear, send a short message:

> Hello! I'd like to book a Teotihuacán sunrise balloon ride for [number] people on [date]. Could you confirm the final total in MXN, whether the basket is shared or private, the pickup or check-in location, what the price includes and the refund rules if the flight is cancelled because of weather? Please also confirm whether return transport to Mexico City is included. Thank you!

Ask for the answers in writing, so you can point to them if the listing turns out to be ambiguous.

## What happens if your Teotihuacán balloon ride is cancelled by bad weather?

Read this condition most carefully. Flights can be delayed, shortened or cancelled when wind, rain or fog make flying unsafe. The pilot or operator makes that call, and passengers should never pressure the crew to launch.

A free new date is not the same as a full refund. An operator can offer another morning while keeping what it charged for transport, breakfast or other services you already used. Promotional tickets can carry stricter terms than standard ones.

### Ask these four questions before paying

1. If the pilot cancels for weather, can I choose money back instead of a new date?
2. If I cannot stay an extra day in Mexico City, do I qualify for a refund? Do I need to show proof of travel?
3. If the ride is part of a package, are transport and breakfast refunded, kept or provided again?
4. If the flight is delayed and I have to leave, what happens to my payment?

Also ask how early the operator announces its weather decision, and whether a rescheduled flight has an expiry date.

## What happens if you miss pickup or check-in?

A pre-dawn pickup is less forgiving than a museum's opening hours. The operator has crews, a launch window and other passengers to coordinate.

[WE FLY's policies](https://wefly.com.mx/en/politicas-y-restricciones/), for example, say the van leaves at the stated time without exception. A passenger who misses it has to reach the launch site on their own; one who does not arrive counts as a no-show, with no new date or refund.

To cut the risk:

- Check the exact pickup pin and departure time the evening before.
- If your booking has no hotel pickup, leave extra time to reach the meeting point.
- Keep your phone charged and on for messages from the operator.
- Save the operator's flight-day contact number before you travel.
- Make sure everyone in your group knows the check-in deadline.

If your hotel concierge or a driver arranges your transport, confirm who brings you back to Mexico City after the flight.

## How long should you allow for the whole experience?

The flight is only part of the morning. You also register, watch the balloons inflate, sometimes wait for the wind to settle, ride back from the landing field, eat breakfast and travel back to the city.

As a planning reference, [WE FLY's published itinerary](https://wefly.com.mx/en/schedules/) runs from an early check-in through balloon preparation, the sunrise flight and landing to breakfast back at its facilities. Exact times vary with the season, the weather and the package.

Don't book a non-refundable flight out of Mexico City or a fixed appointment right after the balloon ride. The time in the air is about 30 to 60 minutes, but the whole outing takes much longer with road transfers or a visit to the archaeological zone. ## Is the archaeological-site ticket included?

Flying above the Teotihuacán valley and walking into the archaeological zone are two different things. Some packages include a ground visit to the pyramids; a flight-only ticket can end before your own visit begins. Entry to the zone is MX$210 for foreign visitors in 2026, paid at the ticket office if your package does not include it.

Check whether your booking covers:

- The balloon flight only.
- Guided sightseeing or free time on your own.
- Admission to the archaeological site.
- Transport between the balloon base and the entrance to the zone.
- Return transport to Mexico City after sightseeing.

The wind steers the balloon. A "pyramids flight" does not guarantee a pass directly over the Pyramid of the Sun, the Pyramid of the Moon or any other landmark. Our [balloon and pyramids tour page](/teotihuacan-balloon-and-pyramids-tour/) compares the packages that include the ground visit.

## Booking with children or accessibility requirements

A balloon ride is not for every passenger. Minimum ages, height limits, weight surcharges and health rules differ by operator. [HappyFly's FAQ](https://happyfly.mx/en/prices/) says children over 6 can fly with an adult, while [WE FLY's policies](https://wefly.com.mx/en/politicas-y-restricciones/) recommend that children be at least 5 years old and 1.10 m tall. Neither figure is a rule for the whole valley; our [kids guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-rides-with-kids/) compares the operators.

If anyone in your group is pregnant, has a relevant medical condition, a recent injury or operation, cannot stand for the length of the flight, or needs help getting around, contact the operator before making a non-refundable booking. The operator decides on its own boarding and safety rules; talk to a doctor about any medical clearance. Weight rules are in our [weight limits guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-weight-limits/).

### What to bring on the morning

Keep it simple: your booking confirmation, photo ID, the payment card if your operator asks for it, layers for a cold sunrise, comfortable closed-toe shoes and a charged phone. Leave bulky bags behind, and ask about storage if you are carrying suitcases. Our [what to wear guide](/guides/what-to-wear-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/) has the details.

## Teotihuacán balloon booking mistakes to avoid

These are the common slips that turn a simple booking into a stressful morning:

1. **Paying for two shared seats and expecting a private basket.** Check for the words *private* or *shared*.
2. **Comparing prices without transfers.** A cheap flight-only fare can cost more in the end once transport is added.
3. **Assuming every hotel is in the pickup zone.** A tour may collect from one central meeting point only.
4. **Believing a weather cancellation means a full refund.** Read what happens to extras and promotional fares.
5. **Not reading the confirmation.** A wrong name, date or pickup point causes trouble at check-in.
6. **Planning the next leg too tightly.** Weather and traffic can delay your return.
7. **Expecting to fly directly over a particular pyramid.** The balloon goes where the wind takes it.
8. **Going to the ruins instead of the operator's check-in.** Use the address on your ticket.

## Frequently asked questions about booking balloon tickets

### Do I need to book a Teotihuacán hot air balloon ride in advance?

Yes, if your dates are fixed, especially for weekends, holidays and private baskets. WE FLY recommends booking one or two weeks ahead, and a month ahead for long weekends. Last-minute seats depend on the company and the date, so don't count on buying one on arrival.

### Can I book a Teotihuacán balloon ride online?

Yes. Operators and tour marketplaces take online bookings, and some operators also book by WhatsApp, phone or contact form. Use the operator's official contact details, check the company's identity and keep written proof of payment and the reservation.

### Is a balloon ticket refundable if it rains?

Not always. HappyFly offers a full refund or a new date; WE FLY and Vuelo en Globo mainly offer a new date, and WE FLY's policies limit weather refunds to international passengers on the standard fare. Transport and extras may be excluded. On marketplace listings with free cancellation you can still cancel for a full refund up to 24 hours before the start.

### Can I reschedule my balloon flight for free?

Usually, when the operator cancels for weather. A change you ask for yourself has deadlines and fees: WE FLY changes are free at least 72 hours ahead, HappyFly cancellations are free up to 48 hours ahead. Check the policy before paying.

### Can I book a ride for two without sharing the basket?

Yes, choose a flight sold as private for two. Two ordinary tickets get you two seats, not the whole basket. Our [balloon ride for two guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-for-two/) compares the prices.

### Do balloon rides include pickup from Mexico City?

Not automatically. Some packages include pickup, some sell it as an add-on, and some expect you to get to the balloon base yourself. The pickup zone or meeting point also varies.

### What happens if I am late for the pickup?

Under some operators' terms the van leaves without you and the booking counts as a no-show. If you are running late, call the flight-day number at once, but don't expect the operator to hold the van or the launch.

### Do I need to print my ticket?

Many operators accept a confirmation on your phone, but they may still require photo ID or the card you paid with. Check the requirements on your ticket.

### Is a ticket to the archaeological zone included in the balloon ride?

Only when the package says so. The flight and the visit to the ruins are separate activities, even when one tour sells them together.

### Can I book a balloon ride for tomorrow morning?

Possibly, if an operator has seats and can confirm your transport and check-in in time. Watch for late-booking rules: WE FLY and Vuelo en Globo do not allow changes on bookings made within 72 hours of the flight. An online enquiry is not a confirmed place until the operator accepts the reservation.

## The booking checklist: five things to get in writing

Before you confirm payment, you should be able to say yes to all five:

- I know whether the basket is shared or private and exactly how many people are booked.
- I know the total cost, including any transport, meals and extras I need.
- I have the correct pickup or check-in address, the time and the operator's contact details.
- I understand the weather, cancellation, rescheduling and no-show rules for my ticket.
- I will receive a written confirmation with the passenger names, date and services purchased.

A sunrise flight over Teotihuacán can be the high point of a Mexico City trip. Book the option that fits your budget, your schedule and how much a weather change would cost you, which is not always the lowest headline price.
