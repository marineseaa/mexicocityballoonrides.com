For a couple, "for two" can mean two very different bookings. Two tickets in a shared basket put you beside up to a dozen other passengers; a private basket gives the pair of you the whole basket with the pilot. Shared seats cost about half as much, and the sunrise, the pyramids and the other balloons are the same from either.

The balloons launch around sunrise from fields near the archaeological zone, roughly 45 to 50 km by road northeast of Mexico City. On a good morning you see the Pyramid of the Sun, the Pyramid of the Moon and dozens of other balloons across the valley. The wind sets the route, so no operator can promise a flight directly over a particular pyramid.

> **Quick answer:** Booked direct with the operator, two shared seats cost MXN 3,980 to 4,200 for the couple and a private basket for two from MXN 7,500 to 9,000 (operator websites, checked 2 November 2026). On the marketplaces this site covers, prices are in US dollars: a shared flight with breakfast and Mexico City pickup costs about $148 per person, and a private sunrise package for two about $1,017 per couple. Transfers, breakfast, photos and admission to the archaeological zone differ by package, so check the final quote before you pay.

## Shared vs private balloon ride: which is better for a couple?

### Shared basket: best for most couples on a budget

With a shared flight, you book two places in a larger basket alongside other travelers and the pilot. It can still feel romantic: the early light and the sight of other balloons rising over the valley are the reasons people book the flight in the first place.

A shared flight suits you if you:

- Want the lowest overall price for two.
- Don't mind other passengers being nearby.
- Prefer to spend the difference on a good meal or a visit to the archaeological site afterward.
- Are in Mexico City for a few days and want a memorable half-day outing.

The trade-off is space and privacy. Depending on the basket's compartments and how the pilot balances the weight, you may not stand side by side for the whole flight. Ask the operator to put you in the same compartment, and expect the pilot to have the final say for safety.

### Private basket: best for anniversaries, proposals and quiet moments

A private balloon gives the couple the passenger space to themselves, with the pilot and any crew the flight needs. It feels more personal, and it makes a surprise, photos together or a celebration much easier to arrange.

A private flight makes sense if you:

- Are proposing or celebrating a milestone anniversary.
- Would be uncomfortable sharing a basket with strangers.
- Want a celebration arranged through the operator.
- Value privacy more than the price difference.

The trade-off is cost, and operators usually fly only a few private baskets each morning. A private basket follows the same wind as every other balloon, so it does not buy a special route or a guaranteed pass over the Pyramid of the Sun.

## Romantic balloon packages: what can you actually book?

Listings use *romantic*, *VIP*, *exclusive* and *private* in different ways. Read the inclusions line by line and ignore the package name.

### 1. Classic sunrise flight for two

The plain option: two shared seats, a pre-flight briefing, the sunrise flight and usually a toast after landing. It suits couples who want the flight without paying for decorations or a private basket. Volar en Globo also sells a shared birthday flight from MXN 2,500 per person on its own site.

### 2. Private anniversary or honeymoon flight

For an anniversary or honeymoon, a private basket gives you time together with fewer distractions. HappyFly's MXN 8,500 private flight for two includes buffet breakfast and a premium sparkling-wine toast. If you want professional photos, ask whether the photographer meets you on the ground, shoots during the flight, or both.

### 3. Marriage-proposal balloon package

A proposal package can include a private basket, a custom banner, flowers, a cake and a photographer. [HappyFly publishes a celebration package](https://happyfly.mx/en/prices/) at MXN 9,500 for two: everything in its private flight plus a banner with your message, a rose bouquet, a celebration cake and a photo session. You book it with HappyFly directly. None of the marketplace listings on this site describes a proposal package, so on those, message the operator before booking. Confirm availability and the exact contents in writing.

A proposal works best when the logistics are simple:

1. Tell the operator privately that it is a surprise.
2. Agree on a precise moment or signal with the pilot before takeoff.
3. Decide where the ring will be kept securely; never balance loose items on the basket edge.
4. Arrange any banner, flowers or photographer in advance; do not expect them to appear on the day.
5. Have a backup date in mind in case the flight is postponed for weather.

Safety comes first: follow the pilot's instructions about moving, standing, using cameras and handling personal items throughout the flight.

### 4. Balloon flight plus breakfast and pyramids visit

Many couples turn the flight into a longer outing: breakfast after landing, then the archaeological site on foot. It is a good choice on a first visit, but a balloon ticket does not automatically include admission to the archaeological zone. Check the opening hours and entrance fee, and whether the tour takes you from the landing area to the site entrance. ## Can you fly together without paying for a private balloon?

Yes. Book two shared seats on the same flight and make sure both names are on the reservation. The operator assigns you to a balloon as part of its flight plan. If standing together matters, ask before you pay; the basket layout and balancing mean exact positions cannot always be guaranteed.

If you are traveling with another couple, compare four shared seats with a small private group flight. Some operators price private baskets per passenger (WE FLY charges MXN 3,750 per person), while others sell a fixed price per group. The better value depends on the size of your party and what each price includes.

## How to get to the balloon launch from Mexico City

The launch fields are in the Teotihuacán area, not in central Mexico City, and a sunrise flight means a very early start.

You have three main options:

**Book a package with round-trip transfer.** The simplest option if you are staying in Roma, Condesa, the Centro Histórico or along Reforma. Check whether the operator collects you from your hotel or from a central meeting point. A listing can include shared transport even when the balloon is private, or the other way round.

**Arrange a private driver.** Convenient for a special occasion, an out-of-the-way hotel, or if you want to keep sightseeing on your own afterward. Agree the pickup time, waiting time and return fare in advance.

**Drive yourself.** Operators have their own check-in site, or *globopuerto*, with directions and parking. Ask for the exact map pin, because there are several launch sites in the valley.

Hotel pickups usually run between about 4:30 and 5:30 a.m., and check-in at the balloon port is around 5:30, depending on the operator and the season. The road is about 45 to 50 km, roughly 45 to 60 minutes before dawn traffic builds. Leave yourself a margin; a precise traffic estimate at that hour is guesswork. Our guide to [getting to the Teotihuacán balloon port](/guides/how-to-get-to-teotihuacan-balloon-port/) covers each route.

## What happens on the morning of your couple's flight?

Operators run slightly different schedules, but a typical morning goes like this:

1. **Before dawn:** arrive at the launch site or meet your transfer driver in Mexico City.
2. **Check-in:** show your booking confirmation and photo ID and fill in any passenger forms.
3. **Balloon inflation:** watch the envelope fill while the crew prepares the basket and burner.
4. **Safety briefing:** the pilot explains boarding, where to stand and the landing position.
5. **Sunrise flight:** about 30 to 60 minutes in the air, depending on the operator and the wind; HappyFly states 45 to 60 minutes.
6. **Landing and return:** the ground crew meets the balloon and drives you back to the reception or breakfast area.
7. **Extras:** breakfast, photos or a visit to the archaeological site.

From Mexico City, plan on most of the morning; a combined pyramids tour runs longer, and the Zuyuá private package lists about 6.5 hours. If you have a flight, a train or a booked activity later that day, leave a generous buffer.

## Is a private balloon ride safer than a shared one?

A private basket is a privacy choice, not a safety upgrade. What matters is the operator's authorization, the pilot's licence, how the equipment is maintained, passenger insurance, weather decisions and the briefing.

Before booking, ask:

- Is the company authorized to run commercial balloon flights, and are the pilots licensed under Mexico's aviation rules?
- Is passenger insurance included, and what does it cover?
- Are there age, height, weight, pregnancy or mobility restrictions?
- What happens if visibility, wind or rain makes flying unsafe?
- Can they send the cancellation and rescheduling terms in writing?

The pilot should be free to postpone or cancel when conditions are unsuitable. Never pressure an operator to launch because you have booked a proposal, a photo session or an anniversary. Our [balloon safety guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-safety/) covers what to check.

## What should couples wear and bring?

Early mornings in the Teotihuacán valley are cold, especially from November to February. Wear layers you can take off as the day warms up, long trousers and closed-toe shoes for standing in the basket and walking on uneven ground.

Bring a charged phone or camera on a secure strap, your booking confirmation, photo ID and water for later. Leave bulky bags and loose accessories behind. If you are carrying flowers, gifts or luggage, ask the operator where you can store them. Our [what to wear guide](/guides/what-to-wear-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/) has the full list.

If one of you is nervous about heights, say so in advance. The basket has high sides and feels different from an exposed viewing deck, but it is still an outdoor flight with a landing spot nobody can fix in advance. The pilot's instructions come before any general advice online.

## When should you book a balloon ride for two?

For an ordinary shared flight, booking a few days ahead gives you more choice. For a private flight, a weekend, a holiday or a date tied to a celebration, book earlier, because each operator flies only a few private baskets a morning. [WE FLY's FAQ](https://wefly.com.mx/en/faq/) recommends booking at least one or two weeks ahead for weekends and holidays, and at least a month ahead for long weekends and 14 February.

Keep your date flexible if you can. A sunrise flight needs calm wind and good visibility, and a poor-weather morning can mean rescheduling. Ask whether the booking can move to the next morning, what happens if you cannot change dates, and whether extras such as decorations or photography are refundable. Our [weather and cancellations guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-weather-cancellations/) explains how operators handle a grounded morning.

## Before you book: the six questions that matter most

Copy this checklist into a message to the operator:

1. Is this a private basket for exactly two passengers, or two seats in a shared basket?
2. What is the final total for both of us in MXN, including fees and any supplements?
3. What exactly is included: breakfast, insurance, photos, certificate, toast and site admission?
4. Does the package include round-trip transport from our hotel? If not, where and when do we meet?
5. Can you arrange our occasion, and what do decorations or a photographer cost?
6. What are the rules for weather cancellation, rescheduling, our own cancellation and refunds?

A clear written answer to these questions tells you more than the words *romantic VIP adventure* in a tour title. Our step-by-step guide on [how to book a Teotihuacán balloon ride](/guides/how-to-book-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/) covers the confirmation and cancellation checks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a private hot air balloon ride for two cost in Teotihuacán?

Booked direct, operators publish private flights for two from MXN 7,500 (WE FLY) to MXN 8,500 (HappyFly), and Volar en Globo from MXN 9,000 per group; HappyFly's celebration package costs MXN 9,500 for two (checked 2 November 2026). On the marketplace, the private Zuyuá package for two costs about $1,017 per couple with transport, breakfast, guide and zone tickets.

### Can two people book a shared balloon ride?

Yes. Two passengers can reserve shared seats and see the same sunrise without paying for a whole balloon. Ask the operator whether the two of you can stand together in the basket.

### Can I propose during a balloon flight over Teotihuacán?

Yes. HappyFly sells a celebration package with a banner, roses, a cake and a photo session, and private baskets from other operators work for a proposal too. Tell the operator in advance, arrange any photographer or banner before the day, and follow the pilot's instructions throughout the flight.

### Will our balloon fly directly over the Pyramid of the Sun?

Not necessarily. The route depends on wind direction, airspace rules and safety conditions. You see the valley and the archaeological zone, but no operator can guarantee a pass over a particular monument. Our guide on [whether balloons fly over the pyramids](/guides/do-balloons-fly-over-teotihuacan-pyramids/) explains why.

### Is a sunrise flight the best choice for couples?

Sunrise is when Teotihuacán balloons fly, because the early-morning air is usually calmest. The changing light makes for good photos, but the flight still depends on the day's weather.

### Do we need to stay overnight near Teotihuacán?

No. Many visitors travel from Mexico City before dawn with a transfer or their own car. Staying near the launch fields makes the morning easier, especially for a private celebration or to avoid a very early pickup; see [where to stay for a Teotihuacán balloon ride](/where-to-stay-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/).

### Is breakfast included in a balloon ride for two?

It depends on the package. HappyFly includes a buffet breakfast with its shared and private flights; others sell breakfast as an extra. Check the reservation details, and don't assume that a toast after landing means breakfast too.

### What if our balloon flight is cancelled because of weather?

The operator may offer a new date, a refund or another remedy, according to its booking terms. HappyFly, for example, offers a free new date or a full refund; other operators usually offer a new date only, and optional extras can be treated separately. Get the weather and cancellation rules in writing before paying.

## The best choice for your trip

For most couples visiting Mexico City, two seats on a well-reviewed shared sunrise flight balance cost and experience best. Spend the difference on a round-trip transfer, breakfast or more time at Teotihuacán.

For a proposal, a milestone anniversary or a honeymoon, a private basket can justify the premium, because it gives the two of you space and privacy for the moment; the route stays the same. Confirm that the quoted price buys a private basket, then decide which extras improve the day.
