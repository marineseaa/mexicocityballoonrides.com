WE FLY, Volare and Sky Balloons México all fly shared and private sunrise balloons over the Teotihuacán valley, about an hour's drive north of Mexico City. Their published prices, time in the air, breakfasts, transport and cancellation rules differ, so compare the exact package you would buy, not the company name.

For the lowest weekday shared fare among these three, start with WE FLY at MXN 1,990, before breakfast and transport. For a couple who want a basket to themselves, WE FLY and Sky Balloons both publish MXN 7,500 for two, and Volare sells a two-person basket for MXN 8,500. For flight, return transport from Mexico City and entry to the ruins in one booking, compare Sky Balloons' Aventura at MXN 3,399 with WE FLY's all-inclusive at MXN 3,600. That only matches packages to trips; none of it shows that one company is safer or always gives a better view.

*Prices checked on the operators' own sites on 24 November 2026, in Mexican pesos (MXN 1,000 is roughly $53 at October 2026 rates). They change with the date, the flight turn, promotions and passenger details, and they are not live availability quotes.*

## Which operator is cheapest for a shared Teotihuacán balloon ride?

There are two ways to answer: the lowest published flight fare, and the lowest total for the day you plan.

### Lowest advertised base flight: WE FLY

WE FLY lists a Monday–Friday shared flight at MXN 1,990 per person. Breakfast is an optional extra on that fare: MXN 300 at La Martina Teotihuacán or MXN 600 at La Gruta, per person. Its standard shared flight, at MXN 2,300 per person, includes a breakfast buffet. Transport from Mexico City is a separate choice on both. They are two different tickets. [WE FLY weekday flight](https://wefly.com.mx/en/tours/flight-sharing-monday-to-friday/) · [WE FLY tours](https://wefly.com.mx/en/tours/).

A good fit if you can reach the launch site on your own, can fly on a weekday and are happy to add up extras before paying.

The catch: if you need a ride from Mexico City, breakfast and entry to Teotihuacán afterwards, MXN 1,990 is not the full cost. Other operators' own sites start lower still, at MXN 1,899 when we checked in November; see the [cheap Teotihuacán balloon rides guide](/guides/cheap-teotihuacan-balloon-rides/).

### Volare's shared flight: MXN 2,900 before transport

Volare's shared-flight page gives MXN 2,900 per adult and MXN 2,500 per child aged 5 to 12. It also advertised a 10% online discount on shared flights on selected Tuesdays and Wednesdays, valid through 15 October 2026. The page states no discounted figure; 10% off MXN 2,900 would be MXN 2,610, our arithmetic. Do not count on the discount for a November or later date; ask Volare for a quote for yours. [Volare shared balloon flight](https://volare.com.mx/en/shared-balloon-flight/).

The regular shared package spells out what you get: 30–50 minutes in the air, coffee on arrival, a Mexican breakfast, a traditional toast, a flight certificate and parking. Round-trip transport from hotels in Mexico City's tourist areas costs MXN 500 per person extra.

It works well if you like a published step-by-step itinerary and want breakfast with a basic shared flight. You can also book it through {{< tour-link tour="171986P1" >}}Volare's listing on Viator{{< /tour-link >}}, which offers a flight-only fare and two transport options. [Our review →](/tours/volare-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-from-mexico-city/)

The catch: the normal shared price does not include the city transfer, and Volare's published time in the air tops out at 50 minutes, against 60 for WE FLY. No operator can guarantee the time you get.

### Sky Balloons' traditional flight: MXN 2,899 before transport

Sky Balloons charges MXN 2,899 per adult for its Vuelo Tradicional: a shared basket, welcome coffee, a traditional toast, a personalized flight certificate, breakfast at Restaurante El Cielo and aviation insurance for each passenger. Children aged 4 to 12 pay MXN 1,990, subject to a height minimum (see the restrictions below). [Sky Balloons traditional flight](https://www.skyballoons.mx/vuelos/vuelo-tradicional).

This one suits you if you want a standard shared flight with a named breakfast venue.

The catch: transport from Mexico City and entry to the ruins are not listed as included. Banners elsewhere on Sky Balloons' site can show a different price from the product page, so go by the checkout total and your written confirmation.

## Which operator has the better all-inclusive balloon-and-pyramids package?

For many first-time visitors, the deciding factor is how easily the early launch fits with breakfast, the ride from Mexico City and a visit to the pyramids, without booking several transfers yourself.

### WE FLY all-inclusive: MXN 3,600 per person

WE FLY's all-inclusive package lists:

- Round-trip transport from eligible central Mexico City pickup areas.
- A 30–60-minute balloon flight, weather permitting.
- A traditional toast and a flight certificate.
- Breakfast at La Gruta, the cave restaurant, at about 8:30 a.m.
- A tour of the archaeological zone with a certified bilingual guide, admission included.
- Pickup in Mexico City at 4:00 a.m. and a return no later than 3:00 p.m.

[WE FLY all-inclusive flight](https://wefly.com.mx/en/tours/all-inclusive-flight/). The same package is one of the options on {{< tour-link tour="247495P2" >}}We Fly's Viator listing{{< /tour-link >}}. [Our review →](/tours/we-fly-teotihuacan-balloon-breakfast-la-gruta/)

Choose it if a cave breakfast and a guided visit to the pyramids matter to you: one booking replaces several.

Before you pay, confirm your pickup address, whether breakfast is a set menu, how long the guided visit lasts, and how restaurant, transport and entry costs are treated if the balloon cannot fly. Breakfast at La Gruta in a package is a table at a public restaurant, not an exclusive booking of the cave.

### Sky Balloons Aventura: MXN 3,399 per adult

Sky Balloons' Vuelo Aventura combines:

- Return transport from Mexico City.
- A shared balloon flight.
- Welcome coffee, a toast and a personalized flight certificate.
- Breakfast in a restaurant.
- Admission to the archaeological zone.
- A visit to an obsidian and handicrafts workshop.
- Aviation insurance for each passenger.

The sample itinerary meets at 4:30 a.m. at the Ángel de la Independencia, flies at about 6:00, serves breakfast around 8:00, gives time in the archaeological zone from about 10:00 and leaves for Mexico City around noon. Sky Balloons' terms give a wider estimated pickup window at the Ángel, 4:50 to 5:30 a.m., so treat the itinerary times as provisional. Adults pay MXN 3,399 and children aged 4 to 12 MXN 2,500. [Sky Balloons Aventura](https://www.skyballoons.mx/vuelos/vuelo-aventura).

It costs MXN 201 less per adult than WE FLY's all-inclusive, with a restaurant breakfast instead of La Gruta and a workshop stop instead of a guide.

Before you pay, ask whether the pyramids visit is guided or on your own, where the return vehicle drops you, and how long the workshop stop takes out of your time at the ruins. A ticket to the site is not a guide.

## Flight duration: will one company keep you in the air longer?

The published time in the air is not a guarantee. A balloon is steered only by climbing and descending into winds at different heights, and the pilot picks a safe landing according to the weather and the ground below, without a fixed route.

- **Volare:** 30–50 minutes for its shared and private-for-two flights.
- **WE FLY:** 30–60 minutes on its tour pages and its private-flight page.
- **Sky Balloons:** about 30–45 minutes on its shared flights, and up to 45 minutes on the private flight. Its terms say weather and landing conditions can change the duration.

A longer published range helps you plan, but it does not prove that one company always flies longer. Nobody can tell you in advance which balloon will pass closest to the Pyramid of the Sun, or whether the wind will carry you over the archaeological zone at all. Read [whether Teotihuacán balloons fly over the pyramids](/guides/do-balloons-fly-over-teotihuacan-pyramids/).

Compare the whole morning too. Volare's shared itinerary starts with arrival at its balloon port at 5:45 a.m. and ends with breakfast and the toast between 7:40 and 8:40. Sky Balloons' Aventura meets in Mexico City at 4:30 a.m. and adds the ruins. WE FLY's all-inclusive picks up at 4:00 a.m. and returns by 3:00 p.m. at the latest. For full timings, see [how long a Teotihuacán balloon ride takes](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-duration/).

## Getting there: which is easiest from Mexico City?

Do not navigate to the Pyramid of the Sun entrance for your balloon. Each operator has its own reception and launch field in the Teotihuacán valley, and the address in your confirmation matters more than a map search for "Teotihuacán hot air balloons".

### Volare

Volare's balloon port is at Carretera Libre a Tulancingo Km 27.5, San Juan Teotihuacán. It runs shared pickup and return from hotels in selected tourist areas of Mexico City for MXN 500 per person. When the first flight turn is full, its FAQ says a second turn departs 1 to 1.5 hours later. [Volare balloon flights](https://volare.com.mx/en/our-balloon-flights/) · [Volare FAQ](https://volare.com.mx/en/faqs-en/).

Ask: is my hotel inside the pickup area, and what is the pickup time for my flight turn?

### WE FLY

WE FLY gives its Teotihuacán base as Carretera México–Tulancingo Km 27.3, San Martín de las Pirámides; its all-inclusive page gives km 28, so follow the address in your confirmation. Flight-only packages expect you to get there yourself, while the transport and all-inclusive packages collect you from eligible central Mexico City areas. Do not assume an Airbnb or an outlying hotel qualifies. [WE FLY Teotihuacán](https://wefly.com.mx/en/teotihuacan/) · [all-inclusive flight](https://wefly.com.mx/en/tours/all-inclusive-flight/).

Ask: is pickup at my hotel door or a common meeting point, and are both journeys included?

### Sky Balloons

Sky Balloons' base is at Carretera México–Tulancingo Km 28.0, San Martín Centro. The Aventura meets at the Ángel de la Independencia, and the transport terms give an estimated pickup window of 4:50 to 5:30 a.m. that can change with logistics; miss the vehicle and you lose the transport. At the launch field you have 10 minutes' grace after your check-in time. [Sky Balloons Aventura](https://www.skyballoons.mx/vuelos/vuelo-aventura) · [terms and conditions](https://www.skyballoons.mx/terminos-condiciones).

Ask: exactly where at the Ángel do we meet, at what time, and where does the return vehicle drop us?

## Breakfast and extras: what you get with each

One company serves a buffet, another a restaurant breakfast, another a cave-restaurant upgrade:

- **Volare shared:** a Mexican breakfast is included, with coffee on arrival and a traditional toast.
- **WE FLY weekday flight:** breakfast is an optional extra (MXN 300 at La Martina or MXN 600 at La Gruta). The standard shared fare includes a buffet, and the all-inclusive has breakfast at La Gruta.
- **Sky Balloons traditional:** breakfast at Restaurante El Cielo is included. The Aventura lists a restaurant breakfast without naming La Gruta.

A buffet, a restaurant breakfast and a set meal at La Gruta are different meals. If you want to eat in a cave, pick a package that names the cave restaurant and confirm the table time, the menu and whether drinks are included. Our [La Gruta vs La Cueva cave breakfast guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-cave-breakfast/) compares the two caves.

Photos can cost extra. Some operators show sample photos after landing and sell a photo package; a flight certificate is not a set of professional photos. A visit to the archaeological zone may include entry but not a guide, and a toast does not tell you whether a full meal follows.

## Age, height, weight and accessibility rules

Do not assume an operator will fly a child because its website shows a child price.

### Volare

Volare's minimum age is five. There is no maximum age as long as you can stand unaided for the whole flight. The standard fare covers passengers up to 110 kg, with an extra fee above that and a maximum of 150 kg. Its private family balloon takes 3 to 12 people, at MXN 3,600 per adult and MXN 3,200 per child aged 5 to 12. Pregnant passengers cannot fly. [Volare shared flight](https://volare.com.mx/en/shared-balloon-flight/) · [family private flight](https://volare.com.mx/en/family-private-balloon-flight/).

### WE FLY

WE FLY recommends that children be at least five years old and at least 1.10 m tall; WE FLY words this as a recommendation. Up to 95 kg per passenger flies at no extra cost; above that, WE FLY charges MXN 50 per extra kilo, paid at check-in, and sets no maximum weight. Every passenger is weighed at the launch site. If you are traveling with a younger child or someone with limited mobility, get the operator to confirm in writing for your exact package. [WE FLY policies and restrictions](https://wefly.com.mx/en/politicas-y-restricciones/).

### Sky Balloons

Sky Balloons sells child tickets for ages 4 to 12, but children under four or shorter than 1.20 m cannot fly, so both age and height count. Pregnant passengers at any stage cannot fly, nor can passengers with blood-pressure or heart and breathing problems, recent fractures or surgery, or spine and knee problems. [Sky Balloons flight information](https://www.skyballoons.mx/vuelos/vuelo-en-teotihuacan).

Send the operator the age, height and, where asked, weight of every passenger before you buy. A price quote is not confirmation that everyone can fly. The guide to [Teotihuacán balloon rides with kids](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-rides-with-kids/) has more detail. For health or mobility concerns, ask the operator and, where appropriate, your doctor: you stand for the whole flight, climb into the basket and take a braced landing position.

## Are Volare, WE FLY and Sky Balloons equally safe?

You cannot settle that from websites and marketing. WE FLY displays an AFAC certification badge and says it holds the permits of AFAC, Mexico's civil aviation authority, with licensed pilots, passenger insurance and annual balloon inspections. Sky Balloons says its pilots are certified, its balloons registered and every passenger insured. Volare's pages that we checked show a passenger flight certificate and travel awards but no statement on permits or insurance. These are the operators' own claims, and no public list of authorized balloon operators exists to check them against.

Before choosing any company, ask it to give or explain:

1. The legal name of the company operating the balloon, especially if you book through an agent or a marketplace.
2. Its current authorization to run commercial balloon flights and the qualifications of your pilot.
3. What passenger insurance is included, and its main exclusions.
4. The weather limits for postponing and who decides to cancel.
5. How the ground crew recovers the balloon, the emergency procedure and the landing briefing.
6. Its rules for children, pregnancy, recent surgery, limited mobility and passenger weight.

A pilot may cancel a sunrise flight when the sky looks clear from the ground. That is a normal safety call. Photos of good launches and thousands of positive reviews do not make any one flight risk-free.

Read the [Teotihuacán balloon ride safety guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-safety/) if operating standards or past incidents worry you.

## Seven questions to send before you reserve

Copy this into WhatsApp or an email:

> Hello, we are considering a Teotihuacán hot-air balloon flight on [date] for [number of adults, and children's ages and heights]. Could you confirm the final total price in MXN, whether the basket is shared or private, the expected time in the air, whether breakfast and the archaeological zone are included, the exact Mexico City pickup and return arrangements, and your cancellation and weather-refund terms for this fare? A clear written reply tells you more than a low "from" price. Keep the confirmation, the receipt and the transport instructions, and check that every passenger's name is right.

## Choosing between Volare, WE FLY and Sky Balloons

On a weekday budget, start with WE FLY's shared flight. For a couple, compare Volare's basket written for two at MXN 8,500 with the MXN 7,500 private flights from WE FLY and Sky Balloons. For a balloon-and-ruins day, set Sky Balloons' Aventura and WE FLY's all-inclusive side by side, line by line.

Whichever you pick, the booking should name the operator, date, eligible passengers, price, transport, breakfast, archaeological visit and cancellation terms. The operator's current authorization and the pilot's call on the weather matter more than any online ranking. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Volare better than WE FLY for Teotihuacán balloons?

Neither wins outright; it comes down to the package. WE FLY publishes a lower weekday shared fare (MXN 1,990 against Volare's MXN 2,900) and more bundled packages. Volare publishes a detailed shared itinerary and a private basket priced for two. Compare the package that fits your trip.

### Is Sky Balloons cheaper than Volare?

Their standard shared adult prices are almost the same: MXN 2,899 at Sky Balloons and MXN 2,900 at Volare, before transport. Meals, promotions and transport products differ, so confirm the total for your date. Sky Balloons' Aventura is a separate combined tour with transport and site entry.

### Does WE FLY include transport from Mexico City?

On some packages. WE FLY sells flight-only shared seats and separate packages with transport: flight and transport for MXN 2,890 per person, or the all-inclusive for MXN 3,600 with city transport, breakfast at La Gruta and a guided visit to the archaeological zone. Check that your hotel is inside the pickup area.

### How much is a private balloon flight for two with these operators?

On the operators' own sites in November 2026: MXN 7,500 for two at WE FLY (MXN 3,750 each), MXN 7,500 per couple at Sky Balloons, and MXN 8,500 for two at Volare. None includes transport from Mexico City.

### Will the balloon fly directly over the pyramids?

No company can promise that path. The wind sets the route, and the pilot controls launch, height and landing within safe limits. You can get wide views of the pyramids from a nearby path even when the balloon does not cross above them.

### What happens if it is too windy to fly?

The pilot or the operator postpones or cancels the flight. Volare lets you reschedule or cancel at no extra cost, WE FLY offers a free new date or a refund of the flight part, and Sky Balloons reschedules the flight free but charges for extras you already used. Do not assume transport, breakfast or entry tickets are refunded; read the terms attached to your booking.

### Can children fly with Volare, WE FLY or Sky Balloons?

The rules differ. Volare's minimum age is five. WE FLY recommends at least five years and 1.10 m. Sky Balloons sells child tickets from age four but does not fly children shorter than 1.20 m. Rules can change, so confirm eligibility, health limits and weight with the operator before paying.

### Which is best if I only have half a day?

If you only have the morning, a flight-only or flight-and-breakfast product fits better than a package with hours at the archaeological zone. Allow for the drive from Mexico City, a possible second flight turn and wind delays, and leave room in your plans afterwards: don't book a timed connection that depends on an early return.

### Can I book directly with the operators?

Yes. All three take bookings on their own websites. Volare and WE FLY also sell through Viator; we found no Sky Balloons listing on GetYourGuide or Viator. If you book through a marketplace, check which company flies the balloon, whether the package matches the operator's own product, and whose cancellation rules apply.
