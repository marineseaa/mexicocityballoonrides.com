You can book a Teotihuacán hot air balloon flight without a guided pyramids tour or a round trip from Mexico City. Several operators sell shared flights to passengers who reach the launch field on their own, by about 5:30 a.m. You still fly with a licensed pilot and the operator's ground crew; flight only means you leave out the city transport, sometimes breakfast, and the guided walk around the ruins.

The cheapest ticket is not always the cheapest morning. If you have a car, are sleeping near the pyramids or have a reliable pre-dawn driver, a self-arrival flight usually costs less than a package. If you are staying in Roma, Condesa, Reforma or the Centro Histórico without a car, the ride out and back can cost more than you save. The sums below show where the line falls.

## What "without a tour" means on a balloon booking

Operators and marketplaces use several labels for different products. "Flight only", "without transport", "self-arrival" and "without a pyramids tour" are not interchangeable: one operator's basic ticket covers the flight, insurance, coffee and a certificate, while another includes breakfast by default. None of them includes transport from Mexico City or admission to the archaeological zone. Decide which of these you want before you pay:

| What you want | What to look for | What is not necessarily included |
|---|---|---|
| The balloon flight, with no guided ruins visit | "Shared balloon flight" or *vuelo compartido* | Archaeological admission, a guide, the return to Mexico City |
| The simplest flight ticket | "Flight only", *solo vuelo* or a base shared flight | Breakfast, transport, photos and the pyramids visit, depending on the operator |
| A flight you reach on your own | "Self-arrival", *llegada por cuenta propia* or "without transport" | Hotel pickup; some operators sell no return transfer at all |
| Balloon and breakfast, no archaeological tour | A traditional shared flight | Admission to the ruins and a guide |
| Balloon plus your own pyramids visit | A self-arrival ticket, then admission bought separately | A driver between the launch field and the site gates, a guide, a fixed return |

A flight-only booking still includes the operator's check-in, safety briefing, pilot, recovery crew and landing. What you remove is the city transport, the meal and the walking tour of the ruins.

If you would rather be collected from your Mexico City hotel and have the whole day arranged, start with our [balloon and pyramids tour packages comparison](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-pyramids-tour-packages/). ## What an independent Teotihuacán balloon ride costs

Ask what your group will pay from leaving your hotel to getting back. The total can include:

1. The balloon fare for each passenger, plus any weight surcharge.
2. Transport to the right operator's field before dawn.
3. Transport from the field, back to Mexico City or on to the archaeological zone.
4. Breakfast, unless it is included or you skip it.
5. Admission to the archaeological zone, if you go in afterwards.
6. A guide, photos or tips, only if you choose them.

The archaeological zone is a separate attraction run by INAH, Mexico's heritage institute. INAH lists it as open daily from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with last entry at 4:30 p.m., and admission costs MXN 210 for foreign visitors and MXN 105 for Mexican nationals and residents with ID. Some visitors qualify for free entry. Seeing the pyramids from the basket does not include admission.

## Getting to the launch field on your own

Getting there in time is the hardest part of a flight-only booking. The balloons launch in the Teotihuacán valley in the State of Mexico, 45–50 km by road from central Mexico City, and each operator has its own reception and field. Don't type "Teotihuacán pyramids" into a navigation app: save the operator's check-in pin from your confirmation. Our guide to [getting to the Teotihuacán balloon port](/guides/how-to-get-to-teotihuacan-balloon-port/) covers each route in detail; the short version:

### Driving yourself or a rental car

The most flexible way to fly without a tour. You reach the reception before sunrise, eat at your own pace and drive on to the archaeological zone afterwards. Volare includes free parking. Allow extra time for darkness and unfamiliar roads, and use the operator's pin in your navigation app. If this is your first trip to Mexico City, a pre-dawn drive on roads you don't know is a reason to book transport instead.

### A pre-booked private driver

Without a car, a driver booked in advance makes self-arrival work. Give the driver the operator's address and check-in time, and agree in writing whether the price covers waiting, a stop at the ruins and the return. Compare a round-trip quote with a package, never a one-way fare.

### Ride-hailing apps

You may find a driver at 4:00 a.m., but don't count on one accepting a long pre-dawn trip, and getting a car back from a rural field is harder still. Have a backup.

### The public bus

Buses run from Mexico City's Terminal de Autobuses del Norte to the archaeological zone during the day. They don't reach a 5:30–6:00 a.m. check-in, and they stop at the site gates, which are not the balloon fields. A bus can work for the trip back after a flight if you know where it stops and when it leaves.

## A sample flight-only morning

Planning example only: your operator's times and the wind decide the real schedule.

| Approximate time | Your plan | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 4:00 a.m. | Leave central Mexico City by car or pre-booked driver | Leave enough time for your hotel's distance from the field |
| 5:30–6:00 a.m. | Check in at the operator's reception with ID | Arriving late can mean missing the flight |
| 6:00–6:45 a.m. | Safety briefing and inflation | Weather and the field can shift the timing |
| Around sunrise | Fly over the Teotihuacán valley | Route and time aloft depend on the wind; no pyramid overflight is guaranteed |
| About 7:30–8:30 a.m. | Land, ride back to the reception, toast and certificate | Allow for this even on a flight-only ticket |
| Afterwards | Breakfast, the drive back, or on to the ruins | Your next ride has to fit the landing and return |
| From 8:00 a.m. | Enter the archaeological zone on your own, if planned | The zone opens at 8:00 a.m. |

A common mistake is to expect to stand at a pyramid gate the moment you land, with a rigid ride back booked. Recovery, the toast, breakfast and the drive between the field and the gates all take time. The flight itself is about 30–60 minutes, depending on the operator and the wind, but check-in to check-out often takes two to three hours or more. ## Visiting the pyramids on your own after the flight

You can book a balloon ticket, arrange your own ride to the archaeological zone and buy admission at the gate. You don't need an operator's tour to walk Teotihuacán. Three things to plan for:

1. The launch field and the zone entrance are different places. Your operator returns you to its own reception, which is not one of the zone's gates.
2. A balloon ticket is not an admission ticket. INAH sets the zone's prices, hours and rules.
3. A flight does not guarantee a close pass over either pyramid. Balloons go where the wind takes them, and the pilot decides for safety.

If you have only a few spare hours, choose between flying and heading back, or giving the ruins proper time: a fuller visit takes three to five hours depending on your route, so a late-morning return to Mexico City and a long walk around the site rarely fit in one morning. Read [do balloons fly over the Teotihuacán pyramids?](/guides/do-balloons-fly-over-teotihuacan-pyramids/) for the view question and our [Mexico City to Teotihuacán day-trip itinerary](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-from-mexico-city-day-trip-itinerary/) for a full schedule.

## Does booking direct with the balloon company save money?

Sometimes. Booking direct can make it easier to ask about the reception point, weather calls, mobility limits and late changes, and some basic self-arrival tickets are easier to find on operators' own sites than on marketplaces. It does not prove a lower total. Compare the same date and product on:

- The final price in MXN, including tax, weight surcharges or fees added at checkout.
- The basket type: shared, private for two, or exclusive for a larger group.
- The cancellation terms: an operator's weather cancellation and your own cancellation are separate rules.
- Whether passenger insurance is included, and what it covers.
- The exact check-in point, the ID needed and the deadline.
- What happens if the weather cancels your morning and you cannot fly the next day.

An operator may offer a free new date after a weather cancellation but a stricter cash refund. A marketplace booking has its own cancellation terms. Keep a copy of the terms attached to your booking. ## Who flight only suits, and who a package suits

### Flight only is a good fit for:

- Travelers staying near Teotihuacán, with no pre-dawn transfer from Mexico City to pay for.
- Visitors with a rental car, who can combine the field and the ruins on their own.
- Repeat visitors who have already toured the pyramids and only want to fly.
- Travelers with their own restaurant or guide booking who don't want a second breakfast or guided visit.
- Solo travelers or couples who want a shared flight and have transport sorted.

### A package is a better fit for:

- First-time visitors to Mexico City who don't know the roads or want to avoid the early drive.
- Visitors short on time who want one company responsible for the transfers.
- Families with children, who can sort ages, check-in and transport in one booking; see our [balloon rides with kids guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-rides-with-kids/).
- Groups without a car, when a separate driver makes self-arrival expensive.
- Anyone who wants a guide and pyramid admission included.

A cheap ticket stops being cheap if it forces you into an expensive last-minute transfer. A package you only half use costs you too. Match the booking to your own morning.

## Seven things to ask before booking a flight-only ticket

1. "Can I book just the shared flight and arrive on my own?" Not every listed product is sold without pickup.
2. "What exactly does my ticket include?" Ask about coffee, breakfast, insurance, the certificate and any extra charges.
3. "What is the check-in pin and arrival time?" Ask for a map link to the reception, not the pyramids.
4. "What happens after landing?" Check the ride back to the reception and roughly when the morning ends.
5. "What are the passenger limits?" Age, height, weight and medical rules differ by operator; our [weight limits guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-weight-limits/) lists them.
6. "What happens if the flight is cancelled for weather?" Ask about a new date, a refund and what happens to transport you booked separately.
7. "Can I go straight on to the archaeological zone?" Ask whether they can arrange a ride from the field to the ruins, even without a guided tour.

Get the answers in writing by email or WhatsApp, and have both the flight and your ride back confirmed before the morning.

## Common mistakes when you fly without a tour

### Treating "without a tour" as "without a pilot"

You never fly a hired balloon yourself. A licensed pilot and crew run every commercial flight; "without a tour" removes the sightseeing day, not the flight crew.

### Booking a "Mexico City balloon ride" without checking where it starts

Listings say Mexico City because that is where visitors stay. The field is near Teotihuacán in the State of Mexico, 45–50 km by road from the city center. Check where you have to be and whether transport is included.

### Assuming breakfast is always extra

WE FLY's MXN 1,990 weekday ticket has no meal, but the standard shared flights at Volare, Sky Balloons and Volar en Globo include breakfast. If you want to skip breakfast and leave early, ask whether the operator sells a cheaper ticket without it.

### Taking the flight time for the whole morning

A 45-minute flight can take several hours with check-in, inflation, recovery and the toast. Leave room before anything else you have booked that day.

### Assuming a weather cancellation also cancels your driver for free

The operator may move your flight at no charge, while your driver, hotel or restaurant applies its own terms. Check the cancellation conditions of every separate booking.

### Forgetting the ruins need their own ticket

Seeing the Pyramid of the Sun from the air does not include entry to the archaeological zone. To walk the site afterwards, plan for admission, opening hours, the right gate and transport.

### Trusting an old promotional price

Operator pages sometimes keep showing a discount after it has ended or limit it to certain days. Compare the price at checkout for your date, whatever an older article or banner shows.

## When booking flight only makes sense

Choose a self-arrival ticket if you can reach the right launch field before dawn and have your ride back sorted. It can cost less and leaves breakfast and the pyramids on your own schedule. WE FLY's weekday ticket is a clear example of a basic shared flight with priced extras; Volare, Sky Balloons and Volar en Globo include breakfast in their self-arrival fares.

Choose a flight with transport if you are coming from central Mexico City without a car. The pre-dawn pickup and the uncertain ride back are big enough problems that a bundled transfer is usually a good buy. If you also want breakfast and a guided visit to the ruins, compare a combined tour before piecing the day together to save a little.

Either way, confirm the final price per person, the basket type, the check-in pin, the ride back and the weather policy before you pay; check your exact date, because prices and inclusions change. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can I book a Teotihuacán balloon ride without visiting the pyramids?

Yes. You can buy a shared flight and head back to your hotel after landing and the toast. The archaeological zone is a separate, optional visit unless your package includes it.

### Can I book a balloon ride without transport from Mexico City?

Yes. WE FLY, Volare, Sky Balloons and Volar en Globo all sell shared flights whose price does not include transport from Mexico City. You have to reach your operator's check-in by about 5:30–6:00 a.m. and arrange your own way back.

### How much is a flight-only balloon ride in Teotihuacán in 2026?

On operators' own sites in November 2026, WE FLY's weekday shared flight costs MXN 1,990 and Volar en Globo's shared flight starts at MXN 2,500 per adult; Volare (MXN 2,900) and Sky Balloons (MXN 2,899) include breakfast. Our [cheap balloon rides guide](/guides/cheap-teotihuacan-balloon-rides/) lists direct fares from MXN 1,899. On GetYourGuide, flight-only options start at about US$136–137. None of these includes your transport.

### Is it cheaper to book a balloon flight by itself?

If you already have transport or are staying nearby, usually yes. Once you add a private driver, meals and a guided visit to the ruins, it can cost more than a package. Compare the total from hotel and back.

### Can I take an Uber to Teotihuacán for a sunrise flight?

If you plan a backup, you can try. A driver may accept the trip, but don't depend on finding one at 4:00 a.m., and plan the ride back separately. A pre-booked driver or the operator's transfer is more dependable for a fixed check-in time.

### Can I take the public bus to the balloon launch field?

Not for a typical 5:30–6:00 a.m. check-in. Buses from the Terminal del Norte serve the archaeological zone during the day and stop at its gates, which are not the balloon receptions.

### Will I share the basket with strangers?

On a shared flight, usually yes. Booking two seats does not make it private. For your own basket, see our [balloon ride for two guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-for-two/) and the [shared vs private comparison](/guides/shared-vs-private-teotihuacan-balloon-rides/).

### Can I book flight only and still have breakfast at La Gruta?

If you arrange it yourself, yes, subject to the restaurant's hours and your transport. Don't assume an operator's included breakfast is at La Gruta. Our [La Gruta vs La Cueva breakfast comparison](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-cave-breakfast/) explains the difference.

### Do I need to book my balloon ride in advance?

If you have a set date, book ahead, especially for weekends and holidays, and check the cancellation terms. Don't turn up at a field without a confirmed booking and expect a seat.

### What if my flight is cancelled because of wind or rain?

The operator offers a new date or another remedy under its own policy. Ask before booking whether you get a refund if you cannot reschedule, especially if the next morning is your departure day. Drivers and hotels you booked separately have their own terms; see our [weather cancellations guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-weather-cancellations/).

### Is a flight-only ticket safe?

Leaving out breakfast and transport does not change the flight. Safety depends on the balloon, the pilot, the weather calls and the operator's procedures. Check the operator's permits, insurance and passenger rules; our [Teotihuacán balloon safety guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-safety/) explains what to look for.
