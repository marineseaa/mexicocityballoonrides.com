Every tourist balloon over Teotihuacán is a sunrise flight. The air in the valley is calmest just after dawn, so operators launch at first light, roughly 6:00 to 8:00 a.m. depending on the company and the month, and spend 45 to 60 minutes in the air. From Mexico City that means a hotel pickup between about 4:30 and 5:30 a.m., check-in and coffee at the balloon port, and the inflation in the dark or the first grey light.

The pilot decides on the morning whether it is safe to fly. Wind, fog or low cloud can delay the launch, shorten the flight or cancel it, so keep the rest of the morning loose.

> **Quick answer:** Sunrise at Teotihuacán ranges from about 5:57 a.m. in June to 7:05 a.m. in late December, and balloons launch around then. Operators publish windows such as 6:00 to 7:30 (Volando en Globo) or a 7:00 to 8:00 flight block (WE FLY's sample schedule). ## What time Teotihuacán hot air balloons take off

No operator guarantees one take-off minute for every flight or every month. Flights are timed for the calm air that usually follows dawn, and the launch moves if wind, visibility or cloud are wrong.

Here is what operators publish on their own websites, checked in November 2026:

|---|---|---|
| Volando en Globo | Lift-off "as the sky starts to glow", around 6:00 to 7:30 a.m., depending on weather and pilot | A window, not a fixed departure |
| WE FLY, sample schedule | Check-in at 5:30 a.m., preparation 6:30 to 7:00, flight 7:00 to 8:00, landing and toast 8:00 to 9:00 | Described as approximate and weather-dependent |

### Is the ride literally at sunrise?

Not always. A sunrise flight means an early-morning flight in the first light, not a promise that the basket leaves the ground at the minute the sun rises. You may watch dawn from the field during the inflation, rise with the first light, or lift off a little after the sun is up. A listing photo of a balloon against the rising sun does not show what you will see from your own basket.

The clock time moves through the year. At Teotihuacán the sun rises at about 5:57 a.m. on 21 June, 6:24 on 22 September, 6:38 on 21 March and 7:05 in late December, so a winter flight starts later by the clock than a summer one. ## Where the balloons take off, and what you see from the air

The launch fields are in the Teotihuacán valley, near the archaeological zone but outside it. Volando en Globo, for example, flies from its own field about 2 km from the zone. Nobody boards a balloon on top of the Pyramid of the Sun.

From the air you may see the Avenue of the Dead, the Pyramid of the Sun and the Pyramid of the Moon, along with farmland, the hills and dozens of other balloons. How close you pass to a pyramid, and on which side, depends on the wind and the pilot's choices; balloons go where the air carries them, so no operator can promise a route directly over the Pyramid of the Sun. Our guide to [whether balloons fly over the pyramids](/guides/do-balloons-fly-over-teotihuacan-pyramids/) explains how routes work.

The light is the reason for the early start. Before the sun clears the hills the valley looks cool and flat; as it climbs, the pyramids, fields and slopes pick up shadow and colour. Other balloons lifting off around you often make a better picture than the ruins. Haze, fog or cloud can soften the view even on a morning that is safe to fly.

### Is a sunrise flight with transport worth it?

Usually, if you are in Mexico City for only a few days. At 4:30 a.m., a confirmed pickup matters more than a small saving on the ticket. Pickup rules vary: Volando en Globo collects free within 5 km of the Ángel de la Independencia and charges MXN 500 a person further out, while Aerodiverti uses an agreed meeting point and returns you to the Ángel or the Monumento a la Revolución, not your hotel.

### Is a private sunrise flight better for photos?

It gives you more room and fewer people in the frame, but it does not change the wind or promise an angle. A shared basket is fine for most phone and camera photography. ## Adding the archaeological zone after the flight

A balloon flight and a walk round the ancient city are separate things. Some packages combine them; a basic flight ticket does not include admission to the zone.

INAH opens the Teotihuacán archaeological zone daily from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with last entry at 4:30 p.m. Admission is MX$210 for foreign visitors and MX$105 for Mexican nationals and residents. The zone's hours have nothing to do with the balloon schedule, and INAH bans drones inside the zone.

If you want both on the same day, ask whether your tour includes:

- Entry to the zone, or only transport to its gate.
- A licensed guide, or time on your own.
- Enough time to walk the Avenue of the Dead and see the main pyramids.
- Breakfast before or after the visit.
- A return transfer after the ruins as well as straight after the flight.

WE FLY's all-inclusive tour, at MXN 3,600 a person on its own website, includes hotel pickup, the flight, breakfast at La Gruta, zone admission and a certified bilingual guide. If a package promises the flight, a restaurant breakfast and the whole site in a very short block, read the itinerary closely.

Fly first, then breakfast, then the ruins once they open at 8:00. Our guide to [what to do after the flight](/guides/what-to-do-after-teotihuacan-balloon-flight/) has the options nearby.

## Best time of year for a sunrise flight

Flights run all year, and every one depends on the weather. November to April is the dry season, with cold dawns: winter mornings at the launch fields average about 2 to 4 °C. From May to October rain and afternoon storms are more common, and an early-morning flight can still be grounded by fog or wind. Our [best time guide](/guides/best-time-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/) compares the months, and our [weather cancellations guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-weather-cancellations/) explains how operators decide.

## What to wear and bring

You may stand outside at the balloon port for half an hour or more before the flight, so dress for that wait and not for the afternoon in the city. Wear layers you can take off later, closed shoes and trousers, and bring a photo ID: WE FLY asks every passenger for valid government-issued photo ID to register for insurance. Add water and sun protection if you are visiting the ruins afterwards. Our [what to wear guide](/guides/what-to-wear-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/) has the full list.

Tell the operator about any mobility restriction or medical condition that could affect climbing into the basket, standing for the flight or the landing position. Each operator sets its own rules, and the pilot's instructions come first.

## Photographing a sunrise flight

Some of the best pictures come from the ground, while the crew fills the envelope and the burners light it from inside; ask where you can stand and keep clear of lines and vehicles. In the air, the sky is far brighter than the ground, so on a phone tap the bright part of the scene and lower the exposure until the sky keeps its colour. Secure your phone or camera with a strap, and put it away for part of the flight. Never launch a drone from the basket.

## What if weather cancels the sunrise flight?

The pilot can stop the launch for wind, fog, rain or poor visibility. A weather cancellation is different from missing the pickup because you overslept, and what you get back depends on your booking's terms.

Terms vary widely. Ruta Mágica gives a free new date or a full refund when it cancels for weather or operational reasons. WE FLY reschedules or issues a gift certificate valid for six months, and refunds the flight only to international passengers on a standard fare. Read the conditions of your own ticket.

If the balloon is a main reason for your trip, book it for your first suitable morning in Mexico City, not your last, so a grounded flight can move to another day. Ask the operator when it confirms the decision and how to reach the team before dawn.

1. **Decide which extras matter.** If you have transport and want only the flight, skip the full-day package. If you want everything arranged, look at transfer with breakfast, or transfer with the ruins.
2. **Choose shared or private.** Two seats in a shared basket do not reserve the basket for the two of you.
3. **Check the pickup address and time.** This matters most if you are staying outside central Mexico City.
4. **Compare the complete cost.** Include the ride back, breakfast, the site ticket and any extras you want.

6. **Get written confirmation.** Keep your booking reference, the operator's phone number, the meeting point, the ID you need and the latest check-in time.
7. **Leave the rest of the day open.** Do not book a tight connection on the assumption that the tour ends at a set hour.

## Frequently asked questions about Teotihuacán sunrise balloons

### Do Teotihuacán balloons fly only at sunrise?

The tourist flights are early-morning flights around first light. Some operators publish a wider morning window, but do not expect an afternoon or sunset departure; check the schedule for your date.

### What time should I leave Mexico City for a sunrise balloon ride?

Most pickups run between about 4:30 and 5:30 a.m., and some operators start at 4:00. Some use central meeting points instead of collecting from every hotel. Follow the time on your written confirmation.

### How long is the balloon flight itself?

Usually 45 to 60 minutes in the air, and the pilot can shorten it for safety. With transport, check-in, breakfast and any sightseeing, the whole outing runs from before dawn to late morning or early afternoon.

### Will my balloon fly directly above the Pyramid of the Sun?

There is no guarantee. The wind sets the route, and you can get a clear view of the pyramids without passing directly over them.

### Can I visit the pyramids after the balloon ride?

Yes, and many people do. Check whether your package includes the zone ticket, or budget MX$210 for it. The zone opens at 8:00 a.m., separately from the balloon launch fields.

### Is a shared sunrise balloon as good as a private one?

The sunrise and the view are the same. A shared basket costs less and carries other passengers; a private one gives your party the basket to itself, which suits proposals and anniversaries.

### Is it cold on a balloon ride in December or January?

At the launch field before sunrise, yes: winter dawns average about 2 to 4 °C. Wear layers and closed shoes, and do not dress for the afternoon temperature in Mexico City.

### Can I book a sunset hot air balloon ride over Teotihuacán?

Do not count on it. The standard Teotihuacán flight is the morning one. If you find a sunset listing, check that it flies at Teotihuacán, on your date, and that it is a real balloon flight; some are photo sessions.

### What happens if fog or wind stops the flight?

The operator reschedules or refunds according to its terms. The pilot makes the safety call, and the rules for the flight can differ from those for transport, meals and extras, so read them before paying.

### Is the balloon take-off point the entrance to the archaeological site?

No. The launch fields are separate from the zone. Navigate to the address or map pin the operator sends; the pyramids entrance is somewhere else.
