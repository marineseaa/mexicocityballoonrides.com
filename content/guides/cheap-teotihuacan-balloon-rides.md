## Flight only or transport included: the sum that matters

The balloons launch in the Teotihuacán valley, not in Mexico City. A flight-only ticket means you reach the operator's field before dawn and get yourself back afterwards. Before you book one, add it up:

**Real trip cost = (flight price × passengers) + transport there and back + fees + the extras you want.**

If a package includes breakfast and a guided visit but you only want to fly and go home, give those extras no value. If you plan to see the pyramids after landing, count the MX$210 site ticket (about $11 for foreign visitors in 2026), any guide and the ride onward.

### Two adults leaving from Mexico City

Vuelo en Globo sells both versions on one page, which makes the sum easy. The Total fare adds transport from the Ángel de la Independencia.

| Cost for two adults | Calculation | Total |
|---|---|---:|
| Zero tickets, no transport | MXN 1,899 × 2 | **MXN 3,798** |
| Total tickets with transport from the Ángel | MXN 2,799 × 2 | **MXN 5,598** |
| Left for your own transport before the Zero tickets cost more | MXN 5,598 − MXN 3,798 | **MXN 1,800** |

If a car there and back for both of you costs less than MXN 1,800, about $99, the flight-only tickets win. If it costs more, the Total fare is cheaper before you count anything else. On the marketplace, the same sum on {{< tour-link tour="171986P1" >}}Volare's listing{{< /tour-link >}} comes out at about $27 a person for hotel pickup, which is hard to beat with a private car for two.

Price the car for your real date, address and pre-dawn start, and make sure the quote covers the wait and the drive back. A flight-only ticket you cannot reach on time saves nothing.

### Travelling alone

On your own, nobody splits the car with you. The ticket stays cheap, but a private pre-dawn transfer both ways can be a large share of the day's cost, so a shared pickup from central Mexico City usually works out cheaper and carries less risk of arriving late.

Ask whether the low fare needs a minimum number of passengers. Teotours, for example, sells its shared flight-only seat with a two-person minimum per booking, even though the basket is shared with strangers.

### Travelling as a group

A group can split one vehicle and sometimes qualifies for a group fare. Check whether the fare buys seats in a shared basket or the whole balloon.

Globopuerto Teotihuacán sells a six-person fare at MXN 9,999 for the group, or MXN 1,666.50 each when all six fly. The page calls it a shared group flight, and breakfast is not included, so you get six seats in a basket that other passengers share. The sum works only if all six people come and the fare is open on your date.

## Six ways to save money and keep the essentials

### 1. Fly in a shared basket rather than a private balloon

This is the biggest saving for most people. You still get the take-off, the early light and the views across the valley. Two shared tickets buy two places in the basket, not a private balloon for the two of you; if privacy matters for the occasion, see our [Teotihuacán balloon ride for two guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-for-two/).

### 2. Look at weekday offers, then check the date conditions

Globopuerto Teotihuacán's weekday promotion costs MXN 1,950 against its usual MXN 2,450, and Vuelos en Globo MX sells its MXN 1,990 seat Monday to Friday. Globopuerto's page says Tuesday to Friday in its heading and Monday to Friday in its text, so go by the booking calendar or a written confirmation. School holidays and capacity can also close a promotion on a given day.

### 3. Compare direct and marketplace prices on identical inclusions

An operator's own site often shows a lower base fare, and a marketplace listing often bundles pickup, breakfast or a site visit. Compare them in one currency, for one date, on these points:

- Price per traveller, including taxes and checkout fees.
- Meeting point and check-in time.
- Transport both ways from where you are staying.
- Time in the air versus the length of the whole tour.
- Meals, site admission and guiding.
- What happens to your money if weather stops the flight.

### 4. Take a transport-included ticket when it lowers the total

For one or two people staying in central Mexico City, a ticket with transport is often the cheapest workable option. The pickup may be a fixed meeting point rather than your hotel door. Aerodiverti collects from an agreed point within 4 km of the Ángel de la Independencia from 4:00 a.m. and drops you back at the Ángel or the Monumento a la Revolución, so check that your hotel is inside that area. A cheap package with a pickup across town at 4 a.m. costs you a taxi on top.

### 5. Split the car with friends

With three to six people, a private driver there and back divided between you can make flight-only tickets the cheaper choice. Ask for one written quote for the whole route, covering the wait during the flight, a possible weather delay, tolls and the drop-off. A cheap one-way quote may leave you paying again for the return.

### 6. Pay only for the extras you want

The basic fare may already include coffee and insurance. Breakfasts, photos, drone video and guided tours of the ruins are sold on top or bundled into dearer options. Our [prices guide](/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-prices/) lists what each extra costs on the listings we review; buy the ones that change the morning for you.

## Hidden costs to check before paying

### Getting to and from the launch field

Check that the ticket covers the trip back as well as the trip out, and confirm the pickup point and time. Some packages stop at the archaeological zone before returning, which makes for a later finish.

### Admission to the archaeological zone

A balloon ticket does not get you into the Teotihuacán archaeological zone unless the package says so. INAH charges MX$210 for foreign visitors and MX$105 for Mexican nationals and residents, and the zone opens from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily.

### Weight surcharges

Many operators include passengers up to about 99 or 100 kg and charge per extra kilo: Aerodiverti MXN 50 above 99 kg and WE FLY MXN 45 above 99 kg, paid on the day. Give the operator your real weight when you book. Our [weight limits guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-weight-limits/) lists every operator's rule.

### Photos and videos

Some crews sell professional photos or drone footage separately. Ask what is included and what the optional package costs; you can enjoy the flight without buying either.

### Breakfast, drinks and tips

A photo of a buffet on the website does not mean breakfast is in your fare. Globopuerto's six-person group fare, for example, lists breakfast as not included. Tips are up to you unless the booking terms say otherwise.

### Payment and cancellation terms

A non-refundable promotional fare can cost more in the end if your plans may change. WE FLY's policy shows how much the details vary: if weather cancels the flight it reschedules, or issues a gift certificate valid for six months, and only international passengers on a standard fare can ask for a refund of the flight. Promotional fares are excluded, and transport and accommodation are non-refundable. Read the terms of the exact ticket you are buying.

## Is it cheaper to stay near the launch site?

Sometimes, and only if getting there is easy.

If you are staying in San Martín de las Pirámides, San Juan Teotihuacán or nearby, a flight-only ticket is the plain budget choice. Ask the hotel or operator for the route to the operator's own launch field, not to the entrance of the ruins.

If you are in Mexico City, count everything before moving out for a night: the hotel, the ride out, the local transfer and the trip back to the capital. Saving on a pre-dawn driver means little if it adds a hotel night you did not want. Our [where to stay guide](/where-to-stay-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/) compares the two bases.

Daytime buses run to Teotihuacán, but do not count on one to reach a balloon check-in before dawn. Check any schedule and the distance from the stop to the operator's field before relying on it.

## Why the cheapest balloon ride is not always the best deal

A cheap ticket still needs the basics: a real operator, a licensed pilot, maintained equipment, passenger insurance, clear meeting instructions, a written confirmation and a weather policy you understand.

Ask who actually flies the balloon, and read recent reviews from other passengers. If an offer is vague about the company, the type of flight or what is included, get answers before you pay. Our [safety guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-safety/) covers what to check.

The wind sets the route, so no operator can promise to fly directly over a particular pyramid. Be wary of paying extra for a view that sounds guaranteed; our guide to [whether balloons fly over the pyramids](/guides/do-balloons-fly-over-teotihuacan-pyramids/) explains why.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest hot air balloon ride in Teotihuacán?

On the operator websites we checked on 6 November 2026, the lowest shared fare was MXN 1,899 per person for Vuelo en Globo's Zero ticket, with a weekday promotion at MXN 1,950 close behind. Neither includes transport, and prices change by date. On the listings this site covers, the cheapest flight is Skyteam's at about $137.

### Can you get a cheap balloon ride from Mexico City with transport included?

Yes. Vuelo en Globo's Total fare costs MXN 2,799 with transport from the Ángel de la Independencia, WE FLY's transfer package MXN 2,890 with breakfast, and Aerodiverti's flight with Mexico City transport MXN 3,200. On the listings we review, Volare's flight with hotel pickup costs about $186. Confirm the pickup point before you pay.

### Is it cheaper to book directly with a balloon operator?

Often, by about $30 a seat at the bottom of the market, but not always. Compare the same date, number of passengers, basket type and inclusions; marketplace listings often bundle more, so headline prices may not match like for like.

### Can I book a cheap balloon ride for two people?

Yes. Two shared seats cost much less than a private basket, but other passengers fly with you. For private options, see the [balloon ride for two guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-for-two/).

### Are weekday balloon rides cheaper than weekend rides?

At some operators. Globopuerto and Vuelos en Globo MX both sell cheaper weekday seats, but there is no rule that every weekday costs less. Check the date and the conditions of the offer.

### Is a flight over the Teotihuacán pyramids guaranteed?

No. The wind sets the direction. You will usually see the archaeological zone, but no operator can promise an exact route.

### Should I choose a flight-only ticket or an all-inclusive tour?

Choose flight only when you have reliable, affordable transport and want nothing else. Choose a package when its transport and extras cost less, or work much better for you, than arranging them yourself. Compare the total cost per traveller.

### What if the cheapest flight is cancelled because of weather?

It depends on the operator and the fare. WE FLY reschedules or issues a six-month gift certificate, and refunds only standard fares for international passengers. Ruta Mágica offers a free new date or a full refund when it cancels for weather. Read the terms before you book a tight itinerary, and see our [weather cancellations guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-weather-cancellations/).
