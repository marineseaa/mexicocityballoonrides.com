For most visitors a shared basket is the right call: same launch field, same sky, same 30 to 60 minutes in the air, from about $137 a seat for the flight alone. Pay for a private basket if the flight is a proposal or anniversary, or if you want the whole rim for photographs; that costs about $476 per person with Teotihuacán Feliz or $1,017 for two with Zuyuá. Before paying the premium, check the balloon line: several "private" tours give you a private car and guide, then a seat in a shared basket.

## What "private" means on a Teotihuacán listing

The word covers three different products, and the listing title rarely tells you which one you are buying. Viator tags all four of the private-sounding tours we review with the same "private tour" flag, whether or not the balloon is private.

Fully shared. You join a group van from a central pickup point (or drive yourself), check in with everyone else, and fly in a basket with strangers. This is what the headline prices on almost every listing buy.

Private transport and guide, shared basket. A driver collects you from your own hotel, a guide stays with you for the day, and the pyramids visit is just your party. At the balloon port you join a shared basket like everyone else. Excellentravel says so plainly on its listing: "The balloon flight is shared in small groups, while transport and the pyramids tour are completely private." Luxury Tours lists a shared hot-air balloon among its inclusions and a private balloon as an extra cost. Arc Tours sells a private-transport option for two, and its listing still includes a shared balloon ride.

Private basket. Only your party flies in the basket with the pilot. Of the flights we review, two are sold this way: Zuyuá's private sunrise balloon for two and Teotihuacán Feliz's private balloon flight. Fly Teotihuacan also lists a private flight for two on GetYourGuide, with no reviews yet.

The test is simple. Find the line in the listing that describes the balloon, not the transport, and look for "shared" or "private". If it is not clear, message the operator before you book and keep the answer in writing.

## What a shared basket is like

Operators do not publish how many passengers their shared baskets carry, and the number varies with the balloon and the day's load. Reviews give a sense of it. One Excellentravel reviewer described meeting "two other couples" and going up as six; a Visita Teotihuacan Mexico reviewer complained of five people on their side of the basket and advised making sure no more than four share yours. Tlatoani Tours warns that the pilot may ask passengers to move to the other side of the basket to balance the weight.

Everything else is the same as in a private basket. You check in around 5:30 a.m., watch the inflation, lift off at first light and fly for usually 30 to 60 minutes, with landing, toast and certificate afterwards. The route depends on the wind for every balloon in the valley, private or shared, and more than 20 companies operate there, so you share the sky with dozens of other envelopes either way.

What you give up in a shared basket is space and control: a spot at the rim you may have to swap, strangers in your photos, and a group pace through the morning. What you gain is price. ## Who should pay for a private basket

Worth it:

- Proposals, anniversaries and honeymoons. A private basket keeps the moment free of an audience.
- Photographers. You get the whole rim and no strangers in the frame; our [balloon photo guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-photos/) covers the rest.
- Nervous flyers who would find a full basket stressful, and groups who want to fly together without strangers, if the operator can take your number.

Not worth it:

- Solo travellers and budget trips. Zuyuá charges the same for one as for two.
- Anyone paying to get closer to the pyramids. A private basket does not change the wind, and no operator guarantees a route.
- Anyone hoping for better weather odds. Private flights face the same weather, and a windy morning grounds them too.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is a private balloon ride at Teotihuacán?

On dates we checked in late October 2026, about $476 per person with Teotihuacán Feliz, or about $1,017 for a group of up to two with Zuyuá. Add MX$210 per person for zone entry with Teotihuacán Feliz.

### Is a "private tour" the same as a private balloon?

No. Excellentravel, Luxury Tours and Arc Tours sell private transport and guiding but put you in a shared basket. Check the line that describes the balloon itself.

### How many people are in a shared basket?

Operators do not publish it. Reviewers describe sharing with four or five others on their side or in their group, and the pilot may move passengers to balance the weight.

### Does a private balloon fly closer to the pyramids?

No. Every balloon goes where the wind takes it, and operators say reaching the archaeological zone depends on the day's conditions.

### Can one person book a private basket?

Yes, but Zuyuá prices per group of up to two, so a solo traveller pays the full $1,017. Teotihuacán Feliz prices per person.
