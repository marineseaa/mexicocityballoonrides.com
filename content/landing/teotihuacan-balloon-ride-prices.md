Expect to pay about $186 to $240 per person for a shared Teotihuacán balloon flight with Mexico City transport and breakfast, or $137 to $159 for the flight alone, on the listings we review and on dates we checked in late October 2026. {{< tour-link tour="366381P1" >}}Tlatoani's balloon flight with cave breakfast{{< /tour-link >}} shows the ladder on one listing: about $157 for the flight only, $201 with breakfast in a cave but no transport, and $240 all-inclusive with round-trip transport from Mexico City and up to two hours at the pyramids. Add MX$210, about $11, for the archaeological-zone ticket, which none of those tiers includes. [Our review →](/tours/tlatoani-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-cave-breakfast/)

## What each tier costs

These are option prices per person, not headline prices, from the balloon listings on this site.

| Tier | What you get | Price range |
|---|---|---|
| Flight only | Shared flight, usually with coffee, a toast and a certificate; you get yourself to the balloon port | $137 to $159 |
| Flight + cave breakfast, own transport | The flight plus breakfast in a cave restaurant | $181 to $203 |
| All-inclusive from Mexico City | Hotel transport, flight, breakfast, free time at the pyramids | $186 to $240 |
| Balloon + guided pyramids | Transport, flight, breakfast, ticket and a guide | $247 to $360 |
| Private basket | A balloon for your party only, with transport and breakfast | $476 per person to $1,017 for two |

The lowest flight-only price among the flights we review is {{< tour-link tour="482323P1" >}}Skyteam's balloon flight{{< /tour-link >}} at MXN 2,500, about $137. The lowest price with transport among them is {{< tour-link tour="171986P1" >}}Volare's flight from Mexico City{{< /tour-link >}} at about $186, with breakfast served at the balloon port, not in a cave. Most all-inclusive packages, including the {{< tour-link tour="357659P1" >}}Viaja Teotihuacan package{{< /tour-link >}} at about $236, sit between $211 and $240.

## What drives the difference

Breakfast and transport are the two big steps. On both Skyteam and Tlatoani, adding the cave breakfast costs $44. Adding round-trip transport from Mexico City costs another $30 at Skyteam ($181 to $211) and $39 at Tlatoani ($201 to $240), where it also buys time at the pyramids. Viaja Teotihuacan sells only two options, so its jump from flight only to the full package is $88 in one go.

A guide and the ticket are the next step. The {{< tour-link tour="333644P5" >}}Excellentravel balloon flight with pyramids tour{{< /tour-link >}} was about $247 with shared transport, including the archaeological-zone ticket and a guided visit of about two and a half hours. That is roughly what Viaja's $236 package costs once you pay the MX$210 ticket yourself, so the guide costs little or nothing extra; the trade is a restaurant breakfast instead of a cave. Private transport on the same listing raises it to about $357.

Season, date and exchange rate move the figure too, so check the price on your own date before you compare. Viator schedules for these flights are priced in pesos, so MXN 2,500 showed as about $137 on the dates we checked and will show differently if the peso moves.

## Extras the price does not cover

- Archaeological zone: MX$210 for foreign visitors in 2026, paid at the gate or on INAH's site. Children under 13 and people over 60 are exempt. Free Sundays apply only to Mexican nationals and foreign residents.
- Weight surcharges, set by each operator: Viaja Teotihuacan and Skyteam charge MXN 35 per kilo over 100 kg; Tlatoani charges MXN 500 from 100 to 120 kg and MXN 1,000 from 120 to 140 kg; Teotihuacan Adventures adds MXN 1,500 above 140 kg; We Fly sets its limit at 99 kg. See our [weight limits guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-weight-limits/).
- Photos: Viaja Teotihuacan, Visita Teotihuacan and Amigo Tours list photo and video packages as extras, with no price on the listing. Our [photos guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-photos/) covers what you can shoot yourself.
- Tips: listed as not included by Tlatoani, Skyteam, Teotihuacan Adventures and others, and left to you.
- Self-driving: tolls and fuel on the Mexico–Pachuca and Ecatepec–Pirámides roads, if you skip the transport option.

## Who should pay for which tier

Flight only suits drivers and anyone sleeping in San Juan Teotihuacán; see our [flight-only page](/teotihuacan-balloon-flight-only/). The cave-breakfast-without-transport tier suits the same people if they want a sit-down breakfast after landing. The all-inclusive tier is for visitors staying in Mexico City without a car; our [pickup page](/teotihuacan-balloon-rides-from-mexico-city/) covers the logistics. The guided tier is for people who care as much about the ruins as the flight; see [balloon and pyramids tours](/teotihuacan-balloon-and-pyramids-tour/). Private baskets are for proposals and anniversaries.

## What a couple or a family pays

For two people, Tlatoani's all-inclusive comes to $480, or about $502 with two pyramid tickets. The {{< tour-link tour="309496P6" >}}Zuyuá private sunrise balloon for two{{< /tour-link >}} was about $1,017 for the pair with tickets and a guide included, so privacy costs roughly $500 more. {{< tour-link tour="5553640P2" >}}Teotihuacán Feliz's private flight{{< /tour-link >}} at about $476 each comes to $952 for two before tickets. Four adults pay about $548 for Skyteam's flight only, or $960 for Tlatoani's all-inclusive.
