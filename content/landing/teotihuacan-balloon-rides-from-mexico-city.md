If you are staying in Mexico City and want someone else to do the 4 a.m. drive, book {{< tour-link tour="171986P1" >}}Volare's flight with round-trip transport{{< /tour-link >}}. On dates we checked in late October 2026 the transport option was about $186 per person, against $159 if you drive yourself, and it includes breakfast at the balloon port. A second option at the same $186 waits while you walk the ruins before driving you back. It is rated 4.8 from 4,510+ reviews. The catch: Volare collects from selected hotels, so check yours is covered before you pay. [Our review →](/tours/volare-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-from-mexico-city/)

## Balloons do not fly from the city

Every flight launches in the Teotihuacán valley, around San Martín de las Pirámides, roughly 45 to 50 km northeast of central Mexico City by road. You will not see the city from the basket. The headline price on almost every listing is the flight alone, with you driving to the balloon port; the transfer is a separate, pricier option. That gap is the whole subject of this page. For the full price picture, see [Teotihuacán balloon ride prices](/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-prices/), and if you plan to drive or are staying near the pyramids, [flight-only tickets](/teotihuacan-balloon-flight-only/) are cheaper.

## Who picks up where

Transfers come in three shapes: a pickup at your hotel, a shuttle from a central meeting point, or a private car.

- Volare's listing includes pickup "in selected hotels" on its two transport options. Give your hotel when you book and wait for the operator to confirm it.
- We Fly sells two different transfers. Its {{< tour-link tour="247495P3" >}}express transport option{{< /tour-link >}}, about $203, picks up "exclusively" in the central area and names Juárez, Condesa, Roma and Polanco, with a buffet breakfast and an early return. We Fly's own website describes free pickup within 4 km of the Ángel de la Independencia and does not list Polanco, so confirm before booking from there. The {{< tour-link tour="247495P2" >}}La Gruta breakfast listing{{< /tour-link >}} meets transport guests by a We Fly-branded vehicle next to the Sheraton María Isabel. [Our review →](/tours/we-fly-teotihuacan-balloon-breakfast-la-gruta/)
- Amigo Tours, sold as the {{< tour-link tour="696822" >}}GetYourGuide balloon flight with optional pyramids tour{{< /tour-link >}}, meets you at the Citibanamex branch by the Ángel. The itinerary puts the coach ride at 57 minutes.
- {{< tour-link tour="333644P5" >}}Excellentravel's private-transport option{{< /tour-link >}}, about $357 per person, sends a driver and guide to your lobby in a private vehicle. [Our review →](/tours/excellentravel-balloon-flight-private-transport-pyramids/)
- {{< tour-link tour="357659P1" >}}Viaja Teotihuacan's full package{{< /tour-link >}}, about $236, includes round-trip transport from your hotel with a cave breakfast. [Our review →](/tours/viaja-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-cave-breakfast/)

## How early you will leave

Pickups typically run between about 4:30 and 5:30 a.m., and the exact time arrives in a message the day before. {{< tour-link tour="394927P1" >}}Explora México's listing{{< /tour-link >}}, for example, collects between 4:30 and 4:50 for a 5:30 arrival. With empty roads the drive is about 45 to 60 minutes, and Roma, Condesa and Polanco sit a little further from the northbound exit than Centro. One operator's terms allow only 10 minutes' grace before the van leaves, so be in the lobby early. One reviewer missed a pickup that had been moved to 3:45 a.m. by phone, which is why it pays to answer that day-before message and keep your phone on.

The public bus will not get you there in time. The ticket window at Terminal Norte opens at 6:00, and the buses stop at the archaeological zone's gates, not at the launch fields.

## Staying in an Airbnb

A private apartment changes things. Some operators collect only from hotels: Globos Aerostáticos Teotihuacán's terms say its transport does not pick up from private homes or Airbnbs. Others are explicit that they will. The {{< tour-link tour="323089P1" >}}Luxury Tours private tour with a shared balloon{{< /tour-link >}} starts at "your hotel or house within Mexico City", and the private packages pick up "from your accommodation". If your listing says "selected hotels", message the operator with your street address before you book.

The simplest fix is a tour with a fixed meeting point. Amigo Tours and the other Ángel de la Independencia departures work wherever you sleep, as long as you can get to Reforma before dawn. Plan that short trip the night before.

## When you get back, with and without the ruins

The return depends on the option. Volare puts its transport option at about six hours from hotel pickup to hotel drop-off, so a mid-morning return is realistic. One operator's own terms give 10:00 a.m. for an immediate return and about 2:30 p.m. if you add a ruins visit. With the pyramids added, Excellentravel returns you at about 1:00 p.m. and Explora México between 1:00 and 2:00. A Volare reviewer who chose the ruins option was given a 12:30 p.m. departure back to the city.

Ruins options do not usually cover the entry ticket. Volare's listing says the MX$210 fee is paid in cash at the gate, and the zone opens at 8:00. For packages with a guide, see the [balloon and pyramids tour](/teotihuacan-balloon-and-pyramids-tour/) page, and for a private car, [private balloon rides](/private-teotihuacan-balloon-rides/).

## Which transfer suits you

Volare suits most hotel guests: one fixed price for the drive and breakfast, plus the choice to stay for the ruins. We Fly's express option suits travelers who want to be back by mid-morning. A private driver suits families, Airbnb guests and anyone who would rather not wait for other pickups. All of them still depend on the weather: read [weather cancellations](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-weather-cancellations/) before you book, and [how to get to the balloon port](/guides/how-to-get-to-teotihuacan-balloon-port/) if you might drive.
