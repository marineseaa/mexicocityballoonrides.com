If the point is breakfast at La Gruta, the cave restaurant behind the Pyramid of the Sun, book {{< tour-link tour="247495P2" >}}We Fly's balloon flight with breakfast at La Gruta{{< /tour-link >}}. On dates we checked in late October 2026 the flight plus La Gruta was about $203 per person if you drive to We Fly's reception, and about $274 with transport and an express return to Mexico City. We Fly warns that the wait outside the restaurant is beyond its control. If any natural cave will do, other operators charge less. We Fly is rated 4.9 from 4,822 reviews. [Our review →](/tours/we-fly-teotihuacan-balloon-breakfast-la-gruta/)

## Three kinds of balloon breakfast

Packages split three ways. La Gruta is a restaurant inside a natural volcanic cave, about 200 m from Gate 5 behind the Pyramid of the Sun, and it has served diners since the early 20th century. Several operators run breakfasts in their own natural caves, which they describe as decorated with pre-Hispanic art or as an "ancestral cave". The third kind is a buffet or set breakfast at a restaurant or at the balloon port itself.

"Cave breakfast" on a listing does not mean La Gruta. Of the listings we track, only a few name it: We Fly, {{< tour-link tour="193670P3" >}}Paseos Olmedo{{< /tour-link >}}, and {{< tour-link tour="484176P1" >}}Arc Tours{{< /tour-link >}}, which offers its buffet "optional in the Restaurant La Cueva". Paseos Olmedo's $350 package stops at La Gruta but does not include the food.

## Breakfast is not always in the price

The headline price on most listings is the flight alone. Viaja Teotihuacan's $148 option, for example, says it does not include breakfast, and We Fly's own website treats breakfast as optional on its shared flight and included in its other packages. Before you pay, look for an option name that mentions the cave, the buffet or breakfast, and check whether transport is part of it. Coffee and a snack at check-in are common, but that is not breakfast.

## Waiting times at La Gruta

We Fly is unusually frank. Its express option says breakfast at La Gruta is "subject to availability" and that "the waiting time outside the restaurant is beyond our control". The drive-yourself option warns there "may be a timeout to access the site", and the all-included option repeats the waiting-time warning. The all-included option's text calls the venue La Cueva, so ask We Fly which cave you will eat in if La Gruta matters to you.

A 2021 Chilango listing gave La Gruta's breakfast hours as 8:00 to 11:00, and reports that the cave is cool inside, around 6 °C. Bring the layer you flew in. One We Fly reviewer in March 2026 wrote that the cave breakfast in their package was not provided, so keep your booking confirmation to hand.

## What is actually on the plate

Most listings just say "breakfast". The ones that publish a menu:

- {{< tour-link tour="456146P3" >}}Visita Teotihuacan's all-inclusive package{{< /tour-link >}}, about $231 with transport: seasonal water, fruit, coffee, artisan bread, and chilaquiles with chicken, steak or egg, or an omelet, with vegan options. [Our review →](/tours/visita-teotihuacan-balloon-ride-all-inclusive/)
- {{< tour-link tour="394927P1" >}}Explora México's cave option{{< /tour-link >}}, about $219 with transport: chilaquiles, enchiladas or quesadillas, with orange juice, coffee, sweet bread and fruit. Its $203 option swaps the cave for a Mexican brunch at Huehueteotl restaurant.
- {{< tour-link tour="377048P1" >}}Globo Tetris's all-inclusive option{{< /tour-link >}}, about $208, serves a buffet at La Higuera, which the listing places right in front of the pyramids, and includes the zone entry.
- {{< tour-link tour="171986P1" >}}Volare{{< /tour-link >}} serves a Mexican breakfast at its own balloon port after landing, listed as included even with its $159 drive-yourself option. [Our review →](/tours/volare-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-from-mexico-city/)

## Breakfast without the transfer

If you are driving or staying near the pyramids, three operators sell flight plus cave breakfast without transport. {{< tour-link tour="482323P1" >}}Skyteam{{< /tour-link >}} was about $181 against $137 for its flight alone. {{< tour-link tour="366381P1" >}}Tlatoani{{< /tour-link >}} was about $201 against $157. We Fly's La Gruta option was $203 against $143. Breakfast adds roughly $44 to $60 per person. [Skyteam review →](/tours/skyteam-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-natural-cave/) [Tlatoani review →](/tours/tlatoani-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-cave-breakfast/)

For drive-yourself details, see [flight-only tickets](/teotihuacan-balloon-flight-only/).

## Cave breakfast with transport: prices

With round-trip transport from Mexico City, the cave packages cluster between about $211 and $240. Skyteam's all-inclusive was about $211, {{< tour-link tour="484134P1" >}}Cielo Azteca{{< /tour-link >}} about $217, {{< tour-link tour="5491014P1" >}}Teotihuacan Adventures{{< /tour-link >}} about $228, {{< tour-link tour="5553640P1" >}}Teotihuacán Feliz{{< /tour-link >}} about $231, {{< tour-link tour="357659P1" >}}Viaja Teotihuacan{{< /tour-link >}} about $236 and Tlatoani about $240. {{< tour-link tour="426035P21" >}}Magic Balloons{{< /tour-link >}} sells a single cave-and-transport option at about $181, with 120 reviews against thousands for the larger operators. None of these includes the MX$210 zone entry unless the option says so. The full spread is on our [prices page](/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-prices/).

## Which breakfast suits you

Book We Fly if La Gruta itself is the draw and you can live with a possible wait. Book Skyteam or Tlatoani for a cave breakfast at a lower price, with or without transfer. Pick Visita Teotihuacan or Explora México if you want to know the menu in advance, and Volare if you would rather eat at the balloon port and keep the morning short. Whatever you book, dawn in the valley is cold, with average lows of about 2 to 4 °C from December to March, so read [what to wear](/guides/what-to-wear-teotihuacan-balloon-ride/).
