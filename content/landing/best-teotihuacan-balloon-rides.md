If you are staying in Mexico City and this is your first balloon flight, book the {{< tour-link tour="357659P1" >}}Viaja Teotihuacan balloon flight with cave breakfast and Mexico City pickup{{< /tour-link >}} and choose its full option. On dates we checked in late October 2026 that option was about $236 per person, against about $148 for the flight alone, and it covers hotel transport, a shared sunrise flight, breakfast in a natural cave and time at the pyramids. Rated 4.9 from 8,231 reviews, it is the most-reviewed balloon listing on this site. Pick a different ride if you are driving yourself, want a guide at the ruins or want a basket to yourselves. [Our review →](/tours/viaja-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-cave-breakfast/)

Every ride below flies over the Teotihuacán valley, about an hour northeast of Mexico City, not over the city. Whether the balloon passes over the Pyramids of the Sun and Moon or beside them depends on the wind that morning, and no operator guarantees the route.

## First-timers staying in Mexico City: an all-inclusive package

Without a car, and with a hotel in Centro, Roma, Condesa or along Reforma, an all-inclusive package makes the morning simple: a pickup typically between about 4:30 and 5:30 a.m., the flight, a toast and certificate on landing, breakfast, then free time at the archaeological zone before the drive back. Viaja Teotihuacan's full option adds a visit to a cooperative with an explanation of obsidian and quartz.

The downside is cost and pace. You pay about $88 more than the flight alone, you keep to a group schedule, and the archaeological-zone ticket (MX$210 for foreign visitors in 2026) is not included. Similar packages from other operators cost about the same: {{< tour-link tour="366381P1" >}}Tlatoani's all-inclusive{{< /tour-link >}} was about $240 and {{< tour-link tour="482323P1" >}}Skyteam's flight with cave breakfast and transport{{< /tour-link >}} about $211. Our page on [rides with pickup from Mexico City](/teotihuacan-balloon-rides-from-mexico-city/) covers pickup zones and timings.

## Budget travelers and self-drivers: flight only

If you have a car or are sleeping near the pyramids, a flight-only option is the lowest-cost way up. Of the flights we review, Skyteam's flight-only option was the lowest at MXN 2,500, about $137 per person. You get the location of the balloon port in advance and make your own way there before dawn. Breakfast and transport are not included, though you can add the cave breakfast for about $181 in total.

The catch is the drive. Public buses to Teotihuacán start too late for a sunrise check-in and stop at the archaeological-zone gates, not at the launch fields, so flight only works for drivers and for people staying locally. Our [flight-only page](/teotihuacan-balloon-flight-only/) lists every option and what each includes. [Our Skyteam review →](/tours/skyteam-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-natural-cave/)

## History-first visitors: balloon plus a guided pyramids tour

Most packages give you free time at the ruins but no guide and no ticket. The {{< tour-link tour="333644P5" >}}Excellentravel balloon flight with pyramids tour{{< /tour-link >}} includes both: entrance to the archaeological zone, a guided visit of about two and a half hours with a SECTUR-certified guide, and breakfast at a local restaurant. With shared transport it was about $247 per person; with private transport and a private guided tour, about $357. That is roughly what a cave-breakfast package plus the MX$210 ticket comes to, with a guide added. It has 123 reviews, far fewer than the big operators, and the listing asks for at least moderate fitness. Our [balloon and pyramids tour page](/teotihuacan-balloon-and-pyramids-tour/) compares the guided options. [Our review →](/tours/excellentravel-balloon-flight-private-transport-pyramids/)

The Pyramid of the Sun cannot be climbed, and access to the Pyramid of the Moon was closed again in April 2026 with no reopening announced that we could find.

## Breakfast at La Gruta

La Gruta is a restaurant inside a natural volcanic cave, behind the Pyramid of the Sun. Several listings serve breakfast in a cave restaurant; We Fly and Paseos Olmedo name La Gruta. The {{< tour-link tour="247495P2" >}}We Fly flight with breakfast at La Gruta{{< /tour-link >}} was about $203 per person without transport, about $231 for its all-included option from Mexico City and about $274 for an express return to the city after breakfast. The listing warns that waiting time outside the restaurant is beyond its control. We Fly sets its maximum weight at 99 kg, with a penalty above it. More in [balloon rides with breakfast](/teotihuacan-balloon-rides-with-breakfast/). [Our review →](/tours/we-fly-teotihuacan-balloon-breakfast-la-gruta/)

## Couples and celebrations: a private basket

Shared baskets carry a group of strangers. For a proposal or an anniversary, the {{< tour-link tour="309496P6" >}}Zuyuá private sunrise balloon for two{{< /tour-link >}} was about $1,017 for the pair, with pickup, breakfast, a certified guide at Teotihuacán and tickets included. It has 41 reviews. {{< tour-link tour="5553640P2" >}}Teotihuacán Feliz's private balloon flight{{< /tour-link >}} was about $476 per person with private transport and breakfast, though its listing excludes the archaeological-zone ticket. Either way you pay several times the shared price. See [private balloon rides](/private-teotihuacan-balloon-rides/), or our [balloon ride for two](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-for-two/) guide for couple prices booked direct and a celebration package. [Our Zuyuá review →](/tours/zuyua-private-sunrise-balloon-for-two/)

## Booking on GetYourGuide with flexible options

If you would rather book on GetYourGuide, {{< tour-link tour="696822" >}}Amigo Tours' Teotihuacán balloon flight with optional pyramids tour{{< /tour-link >}} sells four options on one listing: flight only, flight with breakfast, flight with breakfast and transport, and a full day adding a guided visit to Teotihuacán and the Basilica of Guadalupe with entrance. Prices started from $148, cancellation is free up to 24 hours ahead and you can reserve now and pay later. Its rating is lower, 4.6 from 1,321 reviews. The self-drive option meets at the Balloon's Paradise port in San Martín de las Pirámides; a Balloons Paradise balloon made an emergency landing beside the Pyramid of the Sun on 21 April 2026, and AFAC and SICT said they were investigating. Our [safety guide](/guides/teotihuacan-balloon-ride-safety/) explains what to check before you book any operator. [Our review →](/tours/amigo-tours-teotihuacan-balloon-flight-optional-pyramids/)
